# A file's own colour only where its pair reads, a floor for every text of the entry, and the rest of the review of PR 152

Branch `entry-floors`, written on `main` at `7d67cd5` and rebased onto `8c03ad3`. One commit, not pushed. The kit and the app only: nothing in
`crates/jellymoon-core` or `crates/jellymoon`, and no art code.

The review of PR 152 (its last comment) found 1 blocking thing and 10 others, on numbers only: it was cut short,
made no pictures and ran 2 of its 14 mutations. This change makes the pictures and runs the rest.

## The rule

**A theme file never draws a pair of the entry worse than before for a key it did not set.**
(`crates/sticker-kit/src/theme/own.rs`, `tests/floors.rs`.)

- A key of the entry the file lacks takes the file's own colour only when the file sets that colour **and the
  pair it makes meets its floor**. Otherwise the key is the sticker theme's, as before PR 152, and nothing is
  said. The chosen chip and its name are one pair: the file's `star` and `soft` are taken both or neither.
- Every text of the entry has a floor on its ground. Three are new, each chosen from what both built-in themes
  draw, and each what WCAG 2 asks of that kind of thing:

  | Text | On | Floor | sticker | plain | Why this number |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | a chip's name (`soft`) | `chip` | 4.5 `CHIP_INK_FLOOR` | 5.66 | 5.45 | text a person reads to choose |
  | what is typed (`ink`) | `entry` | 4.5 `TYPED_FLOOR` | 14.39 | 15.80 | text; both themes are far over it |
  | the caret | `entry` | 3.0 `CARET_FLOOR` | 3.67 | 4.57 | a line, not text; the sticker's is 3.67 |
  | the chosen chip's name | `chip_chosen` | 4.5 | 9.99 | 4.57 | as before |
  | the hint | `entry` | 2.5 | 2.80 | 3.06 | as before |
  | the ring | `paper` | 3.0 | 5.35 | 15.80 | as before |

- A pair under its floor is mended, and the file still loads. `soft` and `ink` are the whole page's, so under
  them it is the ground, the chip or the field, that gives way.
- **A warning names only a key the file set.** A replacement for a key the file never set is not said. The file
  of finding 6 (`soft` alone) ends on the sticker's chosen chip and name and is told nothing.

**The blocking finding, in numbers.** The review's light file (`soft = "#666666"`, `dimmed.light = "#9C9C9C"`)
drew the unchosen names at 2.09 after PR 152; it draws them at 4.86 again, on the sticker's chip, which is what it
drew before PR 152. With `dimmed` alone (`light = "#8A8A8A"`): 1.94 after PR 152, 5.66 now and before.

## The other findings

- **2, the field.** The file's `option` is the field only when its `ink` reads on it; its `accent_dark` the
  caret only when it shows on the field. A blue `option` alone changes nothing now.
- **3, the chosen chip's line.** Decided by contrast, not by one channel: a line is drawn when the chosen chip's
  fill is under 1.2 to 1 (`CHOSEN_FILL_FLOOR`) from the other chips' fill or from the paper under it. 1.2 is just
  under the sticker theme's yellow on its lilac chips, 1.22, which is told by its fill and keeps no line. The
  coordinator's ruling said "the chosen chip and what is behind it"; both grounds are measured, because the
  paper alone would take the line from the dark file of PR 150's review (1.52 from its paper, 1.13 from its
  chips).
- **4, the middle cut.** The cut falls only between characters (`text::ends`), and the marks drawn onto the
  character before now include Hebrew's points, Arabic's vowel marks, Devanagari's signs, Thai's upper and lower
  vowels and tones, and the vowels and finals of conjoining jamo. This is the one rule of `text.rs`, so `fit`,
  `wrap`, `clean` and the caret's steps in the entry keep those whole too.
- **5, a see-through paper.** Every floor lays a colour on what is under it first: a chip and the field on the
  paper, the paper on the desk (the kit draws the desk under the page), and a see-through desk on white. White
  is a stated guess: the kit does not know what a window has under its page (`Palette::ground`).
- **7.** A file that sets another shade of `dimmed` and not `light` gives the chip no colour; the README says so.
- **8.** `jellymoon_app::one_line` cleans what is said of a theme file, for the app and the snapshot example:
  a control character is a space, and what the kit's `clean` removes (U+202E and the other format characters)
  is left out. The app left U+202E in before.
- **11.** The comment at the head of `own.rs` and the README's long line are rewritten.

## Proof

**Tests, each seen red on `7d67cd5`** (with only the new constants added, so that they compile):
`tests/floors.rs`: `a_file_s_own_colour_is_taken_only_when_its_pair_meets_the_floor`,
`a_file_that_sets_no_key_of_the_entry_draws_every_text_of_it_over_its_floor_and_nothing_is_said` (14 papers:
light, dark, mid grey, saturated, see-through; 4 desks; 5 sets of inks),
`a_warning_names_a_key_the_file_set_and_no_other`, `a_see_through_paper_is_measured_on_the_desk_it_is_laid_on`,
`a_line_tells_the_chosen_chip_when_its_fill_does_not_stand_out`; `tests/text.rs`:
`the_end_of_a_name_cut_in_the_middle_never_begins_with_a_mark_or_a_joiner`; the app's `tests/f32.rs` (a key
holding U+202E and an escape). Not seen red: `a_cut_in_the_middle_keeps_the_longer_end_and_no_space_at_the_ellipsis`
and the rows added after a mutation survived; they hold what the code did and were seen red under the mutation.

**Mutations**, one at a time, 39, all caught in the end. The review's: R10, R11, R13, R14, R17 and R18 (now the
floor of the fill, 1.1 and 1.3), R19 (two: the chosen fill, the chips' fill), R21, R23, R24, R25, R28. One or
more for each new floor: the floor dropped (chip, field, caret), the pair taken though under its floor (the one
place, and the chosen pair), the sticker's pair half taken (the fill alone, the name alone). And 17 more: a
warning for a key not set, no mending for a key not set, any shade of `dimmed`, the two grounds, the ring's
ground, each half of the line's rule, the cleaning, each of the five scripts, the ring from the caret, the edge,
an own colour over a set key (three). **Five survived the first run** and were closed with a test each: R19's
chosen fill not laid on the paper, R23 (which end is the longer), R24 (a space at the ellipsis), the line's
check against the paper, and `star` taken over a set `chip_chosen`. Not mutated: the snapshot example's own two
calls of `one_line`, which no test runs.

**Pictures**, headless, before (`7d67cd5`) and after, of the review's light file and a made-up dark file with no
key of the entry, at 1440x900 and 960x640. Looked at: before, five grey chips with names hardly read, and
warnings for keys neither file set; after, the names read on the sticker's chips, the dark file's names are its
pale `soft` on chips of its `option`, and nothing is said. None is kept in the repo.

**Lines.** 16 files: 622 added, 120 removed. Deleted: `Palette::readable` and the door's second call (one
function settles a pair now), the channel-distance rule and its `CLOSE`, the app's private `one_line`.

## Not closed

- **Finding 9, the visible-character rule's edges.** Let through though invisible: U+17B4, U+17B5, U+180B to
  U+180F, U+E01F0 and after. Refused alone though drawn: U+1680, U+0600, U+06DD, U+070F. Nobody types these alone.
- **Finding 10.** In a field far narrower than the app's smallest page (170 pixels at 320x240), a chip is
  narrower than one and a half times its height. Holding the least width there means dropping chips or a third
  row, which is more than a line or two.
- Core's same rule for a character a person sees, still owed to the next gated change to core.
- The marks of scripts `is_mark` does not name (the Indic scripts other than Devanagari, Lao, Tibetan, Khmer,
  Myanmar) still count as characters of their own.
- The line round the chosen chip is by luminance contrast, so two fills alike in lightness and unlike in hue at
  1.2 or over (`#404040` and `#704040`, 1.23) have no line, as the sticker's yellow and lilac have none.
- A chip mended for a file that set no `chip` may be the colour of the file's paper: its name is read and its
  outline is not seen.
- Text one sees through may read on no ground; it is left as the file has it.
- Not run on Windows or Linux. The Windows lint here covers core, the binary and xtask only: GPUI's build script
  does not build for Windows on a Mac, so the kit and the app, which this change is in, wait for CI's Windows
  check. A real window not walked. The snapshot example is run by no test.
- The tests were seen red and the mutations run before the rebase onto the art removal, not again after it.
