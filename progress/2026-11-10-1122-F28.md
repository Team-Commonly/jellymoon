# F28 · the app draws a page from page data using only the kit (2026-11-10)

**What changed.** `crates/sticker-kit` and `app/` (`jellymoon-app`) came onto `main` as files. After the first review
both got a door: the app refuses a page file over 1 MiB, draws the first 24 wishes, 8 clashes, 12 stars a wish and 8
unknown items, and counts the rest; the kit refuses a theme with a number out of range and counts the notes that do
not fit. The fullest page core can write is drawn whole at 960x640 and up. `[forbidden]` takes `gpui*`.

**Evidence.** `make preflight` green on a Mac without Xcode's `metal`: 135 tests. F28 at the binary: 18 passed, run
with `--features runtime_shaders`. 40k unknown items lay out in 0.05 s (3.0 s before the door).

**What's next.** A second fresh-context review. F29 turns taps into rulings and can show a whole quote on a tap.

**Surprising.** GPUI turns on `serde_json`'s `preserve_order` for the whole workspace build (ADR 0013, point 6). The
