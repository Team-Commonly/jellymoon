# The gate's findings on cleaning (PR 128)

The gate on the review fixes failed on two findings in `crates/jellymoon-core/src/redact.rs`. Both are fixed, with
the two it did not block on.

## What changed

- **Cleaning runs until it changes nothing.** One pass is `pass`; `cleaned` repeats it. Twice is once by how it is
  built, not by the order of the steps.
- **A mark's bracket ends a path again**, as on main. The change that made it end nothing let a secret with slashes
  through after a URL's password. A path whose replaced segment is followed by such a secret is caught by the next
  pass, which judges the rest of the word whole.
- **A named key takes the rest of its run**, and a dot with more of the run after it. An AWS key id with a tail no
  longer leaves a piece under 40 characters, and two keys of one make with nothing between them are both hidden.
- **Shapes are looked for through U+200C and U+200D.** The mark covers the shape and the joiners inside it. Joiners
  outside a shape stay.
- **A PEM line's words may stand apart by any white space**, so a header with a tab in it is replaced in a text that
  keeps its layout too.

## What cleans differently from the commit before

More is hidden, nothing less: what follows a named key in its run (`key/rest`, `key.rest`); a key split by a joiner;
a PEM header with a tab or a line break in it; what follows a `[redacted]` in a path or a link, when it is a long
mixed run with slashes.

## The tests

`redact/twice.rs`: every shape cut at every place by every space and unseen character, and every ordered pair of
shapes joined by each of them, by the mark and by nothing. `redact/planted.rs`: the cases of both reviews, each with
the secret that may not be read, and the sentences that must come back as typed.

Each of these, taken out, fails a test: the repeat; the bracket ending a path; the joiners looked through; the rest
of a key's run.

## Not done

A key of unknown make split by a variation selector or a combining mark is still missed (`reader.md` says so).
