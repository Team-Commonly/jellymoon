# The ten findings of #142's after-merge review

One PR, branch `art-tree`. The art door is not what leaves the machine or what an agent may do on it, so it lands
on green CI and is reviewed after.

## The principle

The door no longer has a reading of SVG. It parses a picture with `usvg`, the parser GPUI draws with, at GPUI's
version, and judges the tree that is drawn. Three reviews in a row found a file the door's own scanner read one way
and the renderer another; a fourth rule for the scanner would have waited for a fifth. ADR 0015 has the decision,
the pin and what stays different.

## Lines

Without this file: 1163 lines added and 1077 removed, in 20 files. Of those, Rust is 983 added and
973 removed: the kit 762 and 829 (its `src` 167 and 464, its tests 595 and 365), xtask 216 and 140, the app's
test 5 and 4. The rest is the README, `smoke.md`, ADR 0015, `entropy.toml` and one line of `Cargo.lock`.

Deleted: `crates/sticker-kit/src/theme/path_cost.rs` (135 lines: a `d` followed command by command), the tag
scanner in `theme/svg_check.rs` (its reading of names, quotes, a `viewBox`, units, a transform and a `url(`), the
constants `MIN_ART_VIEW` and `MAX_ART_NUMBER`, the refusal "it states no size", `NOT_TAKEN` and `MOVING` in
`crates/xtask/src/smoke.rs`, and the tests that held only those. The kit's `src` lost 297 lines, and its budget is
lowered by 67 to 7413. xtask's is raised by 76 and the app's by 1, each with its reason in `entropy.toml`.

## How each finding is held

1. **A processing instruction named `XML` hid a picture.** The one parser passes over it and sizes the picture
   that follows: 8192 a side, refused. `tests/alike.rs::a_picture_inside_a_processing_instruction_is_not_the_one_the_door_reads`
   pins the review's file; the others compare the door with `usvg` called as GPUI calls it.
2. **`cargo xtask smoke -- --help` built nothing.** The build's arguments come from a list
   (`crates/xtask/src/smoke/build.rs`); `--help`, `-h` and anything else not on it is refused in one line before
   a build. The programs' times are not checked: cargo leaves a program that is up to date as it was, and nothing
   on the list makes the build do less. `smoke.md` says so.
3. **Shapes with no path numbers were slow.** A shape is counted as the path the renderer makes of it (a `circle`
   26 numbers), and its box with its stroke stays within 4096 of the picture (`MAX_ART_REACH`).
   `tests/cost.rs::a_shape_stays_within_reach_of_its_picture_with_its_stroke` holds the review's table.
4. **A stroke under a drawn pixel was slow.** A stroke is drawn at least 0.51 wide (`MIN_ART_STROKE`), after every
   transform. `tests/cost.rs::a_stroke_is_no_thinner_than_a_drawn_pixel` holds the review's table, and
   `tests/art_draw.rs` rasterises both tables' nearest kin that pass.
5. **`an_svg_that_could_run_code_is_refused` stated no size.** Every case states one, and each asserts its reason
   (`tests/theme.rs`).
6. **The gaps in `path_cost.rs`'s tests.** The file is gone. `tests/alike.rs::a_path_is_counted_as_the_renderer_reads_it`
   holds the forms the review listed against the renderer's own count of points.
7. **A relative `CARGO_TARGET_DIR`** is read against the repo's root, where the build runs
   (`build.rs::a_relative_target_folder_is_read_against_the_root_as_the_build_reads_it`).
8. **The sweep knew a home by its name.** A run writes `jellymoon-smoke-home` into its home, and only a folder
   holding it is removed.
9. **`app/tests/bundle.rs` ran `sh`.** It starts its copy of `bundle.sh` as a program; no shell is named, and its
   header says why it is for a Mac only. `CLAUDE.md` is not touched.
10. **`px` read two ways.** The door has no reading of a unit: the size is the tree's. This file states its lines.

## Measured (a debug build, one busy Mac)

- The heaviest theme the door takes, five files that spend both counts to the last unit: read at the door in
  17 ms (32 ms curved), rasterised in 1.9 to 2.3 s, the whole page drawn in 6.8 to 6.9 s.
- The dearest single file taken, 50 see-through ellipses under a thin stroke on 204 by 204: 1.25 s.
- 511 lines under a stroke of 0.016 (1.02 drawn pixels): 0.19 s. At 0.015, refused now: 0.74 to 1.6 s.
- 30 thin-stroked circles: 0.42 s at a radius of 8, 64 and 512 alike, 0.75 s at 4096. 99 ellipses of 4096, refused
  now: 2.5 s here, 4.5 to 6.5 s in the review.
- The worst file to read: 64 KiB of arcs took the parser 0.36 s, and is refused by the 16 KiB of path bytes before
  that parse. `usvg` overflowed its stack on elements 5000 deep; the door counts depth first.
- One paint of 256 by 256: opaque fill 1.5 ms, see-through fill 14 to 19 ms, gradient 45 to 49 ms.

## Not done, and why

- `MAX_ART_PAINT` is as it was. A theme that spends all of it on see-through paint is still drawn in about two
  seconds in a debug build. Lowering it, or counting see-through paint dearer, changes what ordinary art may hold
  and is the coordinator's to rule.
- `MAX_ART_NUMBERS` means something new (two for each point of every shape, once made a path) and is 3072. The
  kit's own art, drawn without its blur, is 2260 by that count.
- The tree's arms for text, an image, a pattern and a layered group cannot be reached through the two lists. They
  are kept, since the tree is what is drawn, and have one test inside the module.
- A paint that names an address (`url(https://…)`) and a processing instruction are now taken: the renderer
  follows neither. A DTD is refused though the renderer reads one.
- A `build.target` in a `.cargo/config.toml` above the repo still moves the build.
- The reds were seen as the tests were written against the old door, not as a separate commit.
