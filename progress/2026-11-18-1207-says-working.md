# The page says it is working (says working)

## Built
- `app/src/scene.rs`: while a made wish's run goes, the page's first note (after "not everything is drawn") is a
  plain note: "Your wish is being worked on." over "“<the start of its words>”. What it did will show here when it
  is done." With several, one note: "Your wishes are being worked on." over "“<the newest's>”, the newest, and N
  more. What each did will show here when it is done." The wish is named by its first 40 characters as core
  cleans them, as every note of a wish names it. No time is promised. The note goes with the last run.
- `app/src/wishing.rs`: a look never carries that note over as the app's own (`Today::again`).
- The footer of a day whose logs gave no turn of it is left out; the legend stays while a wish is on the page.
- `crates/sticker-kit`: the entry's ring while it has the keys is lilac, #6F5BD6 (4.98 to 1 on the paper), in place
  of #C2371C. The plain theme's stays its ink, #1F2328.
- The tests of `scene.rs` moved to `app/src/scene/tests.rs` (the file was near 400 lines).

## Decided
- **A page with a clash has no such note.** It is a planned note, and planned notes come before the made wishes'
  list, so it would move or shrink a clash's choice or the delivery note; F37 holds that a made wish never does
  (`tests/f37.rs`, every made wish drawn whole, failed on it at 960x640). A new person's page has no clash.
- **Plain, not text.** A text note with core's title for a wish at work counts as in hand (`working::at_work`), and
  a text note's id shows among the ids the work tests count. The title shares core's words, "is being worked on".
- **The footer is left out** rather than reworded: every wish on such a page is the person's own and nothing was
  read, so it had nothing a new person needs.

## Red first
- `src/scene/tests.rs`: the two new tests did not compile against the old code at first (a lifetime in the test);
  the footer and note asserts are the ones the change turns.
- `tests/entry_first.rs`: the footer was the old sentence. `tests/work_shows.rs` new test: no such note at
  `layout`. `crates/sticker-kit/tests/colours.rs`: the sticker ring was #C2371C.
