`/opt/homebrew/bin` and `/usr/local/bin` (`crates/jellymoon-core/src/find.rs`, `docs/development/page.md`). A
candidate there that the group or everyone can write to is passed over. A harness found off `PATH` is started with
`PATH` plus its own folder and those places. The "not found" line names every place looked in.

F27's 12 pass with their test bodies unchanged. Budgets equal measured counts.
