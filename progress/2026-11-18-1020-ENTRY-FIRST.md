# The entry is the page's main thing (entry first)

On branch `entry-first`, one commit on `origin/first-run-f64-f66`. Not gated; no row of `feature_list.json`
changes.

## What was built

1. **The field comes first.** The field's row runs from the content's left edge to the left column's right edge
   (wider than two fifths of the page at 1440x900 and 960x640, 44 design pixels high), with "Start" at its right
   end. The big character stands 24 design pixels under the field, where it was over the row before: it moved
   down, which item 1 could not do without (its place beside the old field left the field a quarter of the page).
   The chips keep their old row, short of the big character. The hint is written at the size typed words are,
   and the built-in hints are darker (5.36 and 5.37 to 1 on the field, from 2.80 and 3.06).
2. **`Start`** (the kit's `Entry::go`, `PieceKind::Go`) does what Enter does. With nothing typed it is drawn as
   not ready, and a tap gives the field the keys, enters nothing, and is `Tap::Go`: the app's note "Nothing to
   start yet." says what to type first.
3. **No project.** The field's hint is "Type a name for a new project"; the page's words name the folder first;
   "Choose a folder" is the loud choice (`Entry::loud`). "Start something new" stays a choice (F66's row says
   one choice): a tap on it puts the cursor in the name field, as any chip does.
4. **The chips** are named by the hint: "What do you want done in the project below?", and with more than one
   "What do you want done? Choose its project below.".
5. **No legend and no footer** on a page with nothing read and no wish.
