# The day folds

ADR 0030, decision 12. No row of `feature_list.json` changes.

## What was built

A read day opened as a wall: a row for each wish of the reading with its label, a list of what came true as long
as the day was busy, the clashes, and the window scrolled to the end of all of it. Now what the reading put on
the page is first one drawn note at the column's top, a tally, and the page opens at its top.

- **Kit.** `Picture::Tally { title, rows, more, under, open }` (`layout/pictures/tally.rs`): rows of a label and
  counted marks, a chevron that turns, five rows at most and then one quiet line, twelve marks at most and then
  `+N`. The scene's first tally stands first in the column wherever it is among the notes
  (`layout/column.rs`, `tallied`), so every other piece is the piece it was, further down. A view whose scene has
  a tally opens held at its top and is held where it is when the tally opens or closes (`Scroll::hold`,
  `draw/column.rs`); Enter in the entry takes a held view no wheel moved to the end (`Scroll::entered`).
- **App.** `drawing/folded.rs`: which pieces are the reading's, which of them the fold hides, the rows, and the
  scene folded or open. `drawing::Open` holds whether the day is open, with which notes are open and which
  wishes are tucked; `Showing::column` applies it last, and `Showing::chose` answers a tap on the tally with the
  scene on show. Core is not asked and nothing is written.
- **Never hidden.** An open clash, a clash the person chose on, a note of a read wish with a choice or a field,
  and every note that is not the reading's. Made wishes are never folded.
- **Tools.** The made-up window has `tap:<note>` and `pieces` (what the window draws); the snapshot example has
  `--folded` and `--unfolded`.

## What was red first

With the behaviour switched off (the tally not drawn and not stood first, the fold giving the column back, the
scroll never held), by assertion and not by a build error:

- kit `scroll` unit tests: 2 of 4 (the two new ones); kit `tests/tally.rs`: 7 of 8 (the eighth holds that a
  scene without a tally has no picture, which is true before too).
- app `drawing::folded` unit tests: 6 of 7 (the seventh holds the cases with no tally); `tests/the_day_folds.rs`:
  2 of 3 (the third is the day with nothing read).

## Mutations

Each against `tests/the_day_folds.rs`, switches taken out before the final round.

| Mutation | Killed by |
|---|---|
| nothing is folded when the window opens | both tests of a read day (the tally says open) |
| the fold also hides a made wish | both (the typed words are gone) |
| a row counts the wishes of every project | the first (the rows) |
| the fold hides a clash that waits for a tap | the first (`clash-1` is not on the scene) |
| a tap on the tally does nothing | both (the tally is still closed) |
| a row's label is a wish's label | both (the rows) |

The run that killed them had one line of the first test still failing with no mutation (a comparison of places
that took a bubble for a piece of the header); every kill above is at an earlier line of that test or in the
other test. The comparison was then put right and the file ran green.

## Existing tests changed

- `tests/open_a_wish/window.rs`: the test taps a wish of the reading, which a folded day does not place. It now
  clicks the tally first, in the window of GPUI's test platform. Nothing it asserts was taken out.
- `Showing::column` puts the note of a newer build after the tally, so `tests/newer_build.rs` holds unchanged.

## Open

- Nobody has looked at the tally in a real window, on Windows or on Linux, or with a real harness.
- A folded day says which project a `?` is in, and not which wish.
- A folded day that is longer than its window (open clashes, many made wishes) opens at its top, with the
  newest wish below the window's edge until the person scrolls or types.
- The sentence that this is the model's reading is on a read day twice: under the title and in the tally.
