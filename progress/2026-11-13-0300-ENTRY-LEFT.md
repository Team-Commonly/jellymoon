# The entry under the title, its three colours, and the window's acts tested with no window

Branch `entry-left`, from `main` at `e5aeff3`. One commit, not pushed. It is a change to the kit and the app
only: nothing that leaves the machine, and nothing an agent may do on it.

- **Where the entry stands.** In the left column, under the title, from the content's left edge to 24 design
  pixels short of the big character (`crates/sticker-kit/src/layout/entry.rs`). Before, it stood beside the date
  tape. Every page keeps that band free, entry or none: `layout/page.rs::hero` never lets the small characters
  begin above `band_bottom`. A page with no big character therefore begins its small characters lower than it
  did. A theme whose big character leaves the field under 160 design pixels has no entry.
- **The chips.** Each is as wide as its name. They stand in one row when that holds them all, and in two rows
  when it does not, a chip then no wider than its share of its row. This came from the pictures: six real
  project names in one row were cut to "alp…", "gar…", and at 960 by 640 to "al…", "b…". The band is two rows
  of chips high on every page, so nothing moves when the names change.
- **Three colours, each a key of a theme's `[palette]`.** The hint is a grey (`entry_hint`, also the words of an
  entry that waits); the chosen chip is filled (`chip_chosen`, its name in `chip_chosen_ink`); the field that has
  the keys has a ring (`entry_ring`). A theme file without the keys falls back to the built-in ones.
  `tests/entry.rs` holds both built-in themes to being told apart.
- **The hint** is now "Type a wish: Tab for its project, Enter". The old one was cut with an ellipsis in the
  sticker theme's font at every size. `app/tests/f42.rs` asks for the whole line.
- **Spaces are no words** (finding 3). `EntryState::holds` trims, so an entry of spaces alone goes with its
  page (`crates/sticker-kit/src/entry.rs`, test in `tests/entry_draw.rs`).
- **The window's acts with no window** (finding 4). `app/src/showing.rs` holds what was in `window.rs` and could
  not be tested: what Enter is answered with, when a run is asked for, what a tap on an option shows and when
  it leaves work, a look that is stale, the theme key, and the two lines the window prints. `app/tests/f43/showing.rs`
  makes the same calls in the same order with the work run on the test's thread.
- **What stayed in `window.rs`, and why.** Only wiring: opening the window, the handlers given to the kit's
  page, `cx.background_spawn` and the `update` that carries a result back, `cx.notify`, and the printing. Each
  needs a GPUI `App` and a real window, which no test here may open. It has no rule left in it: every branch
  it took is now a call on `Showing`.
- **Tests first.** Each test was seen red before its change. Mutations, one at a time, all killed: K1 (spaces),
  L1 to L8 (the place, the band, one row and two), C1 to C7 (the colours), A1 to A8 (`showing.rs`). L6, a band
  one row high, lived at first; `tests/entry_band.rs` now asks for the room kept under the second row.
- **Pictures.** Headless only (`--example snapshot`): the fixture day and a made-up fullest page (eight wishes of
  40 characters, six projects), at 1440 by 900 and 960 by 640, with a hint and with typed words, both themes.
  Looked at: the entry covers nothing, the hint is whole, all six names can be read at 1440 and five of six at
  960, and the three colours tell apart in both themes. None is kept in the repo.
- **Lines.** 22 files: 632 added, 147 removed (the kit's and the app's budgets raised in `entropy.toml`, each
  with its reason).
- **Deleted.** From `window.rs`: the `Shown` type and `theme_key`, `switch_theme` and `received` (their rules are
  in `showing.rs`). From the kit: the minimum-width chip row of one line, and the theme text colour match in
  `draw/mod.rs` (now `Brush::ink`).
- **Not closed.**
  - The grey of an entry's waiting words, and everything in `window.rs`, are seen only at a real window. A
    person's walk of the window is owed.
  - `Showing`'s tests run the work on the test's thread; that the window runs it off its own thread is not tested.
  - A theme whose big character leaves under 160 design pixels has no entry, so no wish can be typed there.
  - A sixth long name is still cut at 960 by 640 ("garden-let…").
  - Not run on Windows or Linux.
