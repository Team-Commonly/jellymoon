# F53: a follow-up

On branch `f53-follow`, one commit on main after F52 (#173). The account is `docs/development/wish.md` ("A
follow-up"), `run.md` ("A follow-up's run"), `keep.md` ("A follow-up's work"), `toss.md` ("Work a follow-up
stands on"), `show.md` and ADR 0018, "Found while building F53".

## What changed

- **`jellymoon wish --after <id>`** makes a made wish that names the wish it follows (`after`, last in the kept
  wish) and takes its project. It is refused, in one fixed sentence each, unless that wish's run finished with
  work that waits or was kept (`run/follow.rs`, `followed`).
- **`jellymoon run` of a follow-up** takes the keeps' lock, reads the followed record, makes its copy at the
  followed work's commit while that waits and at the checkout's once it was kept (`copy.rs`, `Base`), and
  starts the harness with `--resume <followed session> --fork-session --session-id <new>`. A harness that
  keeps that session no more is started once more with a new session; the record's `follows` and `forked` say
  which (`follow.rs`, `Go::started`).
- **The order.** `run keep` of a follow-up is refused while the followed work waits; `run toss` of work is
  refused while a follow-up's run stands on it (`follow.rs`, `keep_first`, `toss_first`).
- **A keep after a joined keep.** `copy::Keep` gains `held`, the commit the followed work was kept as, and git
  is given the run's start as the merge's base (`copy/keep/made.rs`).
- **Moved, not changed:** a run's fixed lines are in `run/words.rs`, and a keep's `done` in `run/keep/done.rs`,
  to keep `run.rs` and `run/keep.rs` under the file limit.
- **The smoke chain** has a `follow` step (`crates/xtask/src/smoke/follow.rs`).
- **Three items of F52's gate:** wish.md's "Not closed" sentence on a key over more than eight lines, "only
  marks" naming any other sign, and two more cases in `still.rs` (a two-line key id with a word at both ends,
  and an id in ten lines between spaces), each failing with its reading taken out.

## What the next session should know: open rows

1. The fork was not run on a real harness with core's command line.
2. A follow-up made and not run, whose followed work is then tossed, cannot be run and cannot be let go.
3. A follow-up of kept work is made where the checkout stands; nothing checks the checkout holds that work.
4. On git 2.38 and 2.39 a follow-up kept after a joined keep is merged from git's own base and may not fit.
5. A fork that reports a lost session is taken to have changed nothing in the copy.
6. A follow-up that changed nothing says `where the checkout was` also when it started at followed work.
7. Decision 9 says a toss is refused for a waiting follow-up; the build also refuses while the follow-up's run
   goes or asks. ADR 0018 says so under "Found while building F53"; the decision's own sentence is as it was.
8. The rows of `2026-11-15-0215-F52-SECOND-GATE-FIX.md`, still open.
