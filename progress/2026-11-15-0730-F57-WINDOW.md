# F57: the jellyfish is a window, and the app lives with the page closed

On branch `f57-window`, one commit on top of `36d0a9d`. The account is `docs/development/app.md` ("The
jellyfish's two windows"), `hear.md` ("Every sentence"), the kit's README ("The edge scene") and ADR 0019, "Found
while building F57".

## What changed

- **The app**: `popup.rs` says what each of the two windows is asked for, as data, and is the one place that
  becomes GPUI's options. `jellyfish.rs` opens the jellyfish's window where the kit's placing says, in the room
  the system leaves on the primary display, and opens and removes the bubble's. `shell.rs` holds the app's
  windows as one thing: the page's view outlives its window, a tap on the jellyfish or opening the app again
  shows it, Quit ends all of it, and once a second core is asked what the jellyfish shows, off the window's
  thread. `layout --jelly` prints `opens` with each window. One function reads `--size` and `--screen`.
- **The kit**: `EdgeView` paints one window of an edge scene; the character has seven poses; `place_pair`;
  an edge text is 9 to 22 pixels; the palette's `quoted` ink with its floor; `Rect::clipped`; `clean` takes four
  more characters as a line's end and keeps at most 8 marks on one character; `Said` is `EdgeText`.
- **Core**: a line's words and project are something a person sees; a carriage return inside a line is no line;
  `Hearing::refusals()`; the order of states is `JellyState::ORDER` and nowhere else; a hearing says a short
  sentence for no harness.

## What was seen red

`app/tests/f57.rs` before any code: 8 of 8 failed, each for its reason (no `opens`; the bubble over the jellyfish
at 320x322; a theme's sizes putting a piece out of its window; a refusal with the whole path and the system's
words; `--screen` said back whole; unseen words read as a line; U+2028 no space; marks not capped). The kit's
`tests/edge.rs`: 2 failed. Core's `tests/hear.rs`: 1. The binary's `f55`: 1.

Not seen red: `app/tests/f57/window.rs`, which could not compile before the code it opens was there; it is held
by mutations A4 to A9 and A14 to A16 only. `draw/edge.rs`, the poses in `art.rs` and the change to `clean` were
written before the red run. The kit's `tests/edge_parts.rs` and `tests/edge_draw.rs` were first run with the code.

## Mutations

30 one-rule changes, each run against its test: 9 in the kit, 5 in core, 16 in the app. All killed. Two survived
a first round. A form feed refused inside a line was a rule with no effect, since no JSON has one outside a
text or raw inside one: the rule was taken out, and the tests that refuse it stay. A sentence left out of
`Hearing::refusals()` with another said twice passed the app's count of them: core's doc test now holds the count
and that none is there twice.

## Open rows

1. **Nothing was opened on a screen.** Three observations are for a person, and what they find is changed in
   `popup::options` and nowhere else: whether a pop-up brings the app to the front when it is opened or tapped;
   whether the system draws a shadow or a frame round a see-through window; whether the part one sees through
   takes clicks.
2. Not run by any test: that Quit ends the process, the menu, Cmd-Q, and opening the app again. A test sees the
   windows removed, the end marked and nothing opened after. `JELLYMOON_QUIT_AFTER` is tested the same way, on
   the test platform's clock.
3. The room is asked once. A Dock that is moved, or a display that is changed, is not followed.
4. The open app shows `working`, `asks`, `waits` and `quiet`. `listening`, `heard` and `cannot` are drawn when a
   line of a hearing is given, which only the test platform does: no hearing runs in the app yet.
5. A refusal that carries a day, or another command's words, is cut with an ellipsis where it is longer than the
   bubble's four lines. Only the sentences of `Hearing::refusals()` are proved whole.
6. A theme file's `quoted` is measured on a bubble laid on white; the bubble's window is see-through.
7. The jellyfish keeps the theme the app was opened with; `t` switches the page only.
8. If the jellyfish cannot be opened, the app says so on stderr and goes on with the page; closing the page then
   ends it.
