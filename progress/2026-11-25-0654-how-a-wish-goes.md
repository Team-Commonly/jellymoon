# How a wish goes

One slice on the first page (ADR 0027, decision 10). No row of `feature_list.json` changes.

## What was built

- **The kit has one more picture, the strip** (`Picture::Strip { panels }`, `src/layout/pictures/strip.rs`): up to
  three panels in one row, each a small sketch (`Shows::{Speech, Sheet, Folder}`) with its caption under it, and an
  arrow from each sketch to the next. The sheet with its pencil is a track's (`track::pencil`, at rest here) and the
  folder with its tick is the sheets' (`sheets::folder_at`); the speech bubble is one path and two squiggles, and
  the cross beside the folder is the pencil's own. No new pencil mark.
- **A strip takes no tap.** `Picture::taps` is false for it alone: the view makes no tap target of it
  (`draw/column.rs`), its note has no `?` (`layout/drawn.rs`), and the app opens nothing for its id
  (`drawing.rs`, `Open::tapped`) and always draws its one line (`drawing::passed`).
- **The app's first page.** Where the note "Nothing wished for yet today." stood, `scene::how` puts the strip:
  "say a wish", "it gets made", "keep or toss", and one line under it. Same place, same moments: a page with no
  wish and nothing set aside, not while a wish is being read or a sharper wish waits, over a clash as before.

Every sentence, before and after:

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| title | Nothing wished for yet today. | (none; the three captions) |
| a project on offer | Type what you want done in the field at the bottom. | Type a wish in the field at the bottom. |
| no project | Choose the folder you want to work in, or type a name for a new project in the field at the bottom. | First choose a folder to work in, or name a new project. |

## Open

- **Nobody has seen it in a real window.** The pictures are the windowless example's. How the strip reads while
  it draws itself was looked at in one stopped frame of the kit's sample page.
- The clash case is held at the function that orders the column, on a typed page as in `f37.rs`, and not through
  the made-up window: no reading of core's was made that holds a clash and no wish.
- The second panel says nothing of who makes it, and the cross beside the folder is small: a newcomer may read
  "keep or toss" before they see what the cross is for.
- The card is white, as every drawn note is, where the old note was yellow.
- Windows and Linux were not run.
