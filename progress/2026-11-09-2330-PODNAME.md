# PODNAME: hello names the pod; an invite is read before sign-in; GET /v1/session

**Changed.**
- `schema/events.json`: `Hello` carries `podName` (required, 1 to 80 characters). `schema/pod-api.json`: `GET /v1/invites/:code` (no auth, `InvitePreview {podName, inviterName, expiresAt}`), `GET /v1/session` (no auth, `SessionResponse {user?}`) and three rules. `make gen` regenerated TS, Go and `docs/protocol.md`.
- Both adapters: hello's `podName` is the pod's stored name. An invite now keeps who made it (`inviter`: a column added in place on the server's Postgres and in an existing pod object's SQLite). The read answers 404 `No such invite.` for unknown, used, expired and inviter-less codes alike, writes nothing, and has no rate limit, as the accept has none.
- `GET /v1/session` is `/v1/me` that never refuses. `/v1/me` keeps its 401.
- Web: the bar shows the pod's name once hello has named it, the id before; the invite page reads the invite and names the pod and the inviter, keeping its plain line when the read fails; boot asks `/v1/session`.

**Evidence.** `e2e/podname_test.go` (TestPODNAME) was STEPS=1/5 on both adapters before the change and 5/5 after. `server/test/invite-read.test.ts` and `worker/test/invite-read.test.ts` were 0 of 9 before and 9 of 9 after. Web: 9 tests red before, all green after.
