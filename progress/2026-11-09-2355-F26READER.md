**Found in real logs (Claude Code 2.1, keys only).**
- A slash command typed by a person starts with `<command-message>`, not `<command-name>`; the reader drops both.
- The explainer paragraph `(The JSON line above ...` sits inside `<artifact-view-context>`, not after it; the reader cuts it in either place.
- A prompt typed with a pasted image has list content with `origin.kind: human`. The reader drops it (it ends the turn before it). Under 1% of typed prompts in the sample. Whether to keep its text parts is an open call for the owner.
