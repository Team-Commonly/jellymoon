# F18 PR 2: the /pod/:id stream

**Changed.** The pod screen on top of PR 1's store, card model and key map. `CardView.tsx` renders a card (author, headline,
status and receipt chips, a folded body) and, for an ask, its context, numbered options with the recommended one marked, the
confirm row and the ruling line. `usePodKeys.ts` applies the pure key map's one action to the DOM and the store and moves focus
to the next open ask once the ruling lands. `PodScreen.tsx` holds the composer, the "Needs you · n" toggle (a portal into the
top bar) and `?filter=needs-you`. `usePod.ts` binds a store to a component. `main.tsx` gets a plain browser socket connector.

**Evidence.** 30 component tests were committed red first against a throwing stub, then green (68 in the web package). Three
mutants were each caught: the confirm row skipped (6 tests), the composer text-field guard bypassed (1), needs-you matching by
role dropped (2).

**Next.** The Playwright specs (a browser harness and a CI job are needed first). The browser socket needs cookie auth on the pod
socket. The look is a plain stylesheet; the design pass is PR 5.

**Surprising.** An ask card has its own h4 headings, so tests that look for "the card's heading" must ask for level 3.
Pressing Enter on a focused button must be left to the button, or a click-then-Enter would both open and rule.
