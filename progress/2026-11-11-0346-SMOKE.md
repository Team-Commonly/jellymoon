# Smoke · the built programs run together (2026-11-11)

**What changed.** `cargo xtask smoke` (`make smoke`) builds `jellymoon`, `jellymoon-app` and `fake-claude` and runs
them as a chain of named steps in a temporary home: made-up logs, `day`, `page`, `layout` in both themes, a broken
page, an empty day, `uninstall`. `--window` and `--real` are for a person. CI runs it after the tests on all three
systems. `xtask` now finds the repo's root when it runs, not when it was built.

**Evidence.** On a Mac: 8 steps in 1.9 s after the build. A wrong expectation in `page` failed with `FAILED at page`.

**What's next.** F29, `jellymoon rule` and "the app opens today's page" each add a step in `smoke/steps.rs`. A
maintainer runs `--real` and `--window` once; CI's macOS window step shows whether a hosted runner can open one.

**Surprising.** Worktrees sharing a target folder share the `xtask` binary, so a root fixed at build time checked
another worktree's files. `uninstall` is left out of `--real`: with F29 it will edit the harness's own settings.
