# F87: a run is asked to end with its result

## Built
- The schema of the last word is one file, `crates/jellymoon-core/formats/last-word.v1.json`, read by core with
  `include_str!` (`run/asks.rs`, `schema()`) and passed with `--json-schema` without its last line break. It gains
  `result` (`pieces`, each a `type` of `did`, `change`, `decided`, `unsure`, with `text`, `file`, `before`, `after`)
  and the bounds a schema can say: 12 pieces, 3 lines a side, 100 characters a line, 280 a text. Every object is
  closed; only `state` is required. The version is in the file's name; a new shape is a new file beside it.
- The fixed words (`run.rs`, `PROMPT`) ask for the result: a `did` a non-programmer can read, at most two
  `change` pieces quoting whole, exact lines, `decided` and `unsure` only when worth a look, and that nothing was
  run. A run, an answer and a follow-up's fork share them and the schema (`lined`, unchanged).
- `asks::last` already read `done` alone or with a `result` as done (F85): nothing there changed.
- `run.md`: the schema and its file, the new words and why each is there, "The last word is data" brought up to
  date, and a section "Measured on a real Claude Code" with the three measurements, no numbers.

result. 2 of 4 failed; `done` alone and the too-old harness passed, as they must before and after. `tests/f39.rs`
