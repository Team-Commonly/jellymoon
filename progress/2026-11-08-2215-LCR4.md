# LCR4: a CodeRabbit summary in the new format is a gap, not a refusal

**Changed.** CodeRabbit's summary comment no longer has a `recent_review_status` block (PR 30), so land refused it as a forgery shape. In
`scripts/land-coderabbit.mjs`, check (c) now treats a summary with no readable status block as a gap, and a coverage marker whose `kind` is
anything but `reviewed` as a gap too. A gap already passes, so refusing such a summary protected nothing and only blocked lands. The land note
says `no CodeRabbit review at this head`, and the reviews and threads are still read.

**Still refuses.** `CHANGES_REQUESTED` at the head, an unanswered Major or Critical thread, a failed read, two or more summaries, a marker in
a reply, and (with a status block present) a malformed marker or a missing walkthrough or risk block. A comment by anyone but the CodeRabbit
bot is ignored, as before, and can never make the head pass as a summary.

**Tests.** `scripts/check-land-coderabbit.mjs` gained a fixture trimmed from the new summary shape and cases for each rule above. They were
committed red first.
