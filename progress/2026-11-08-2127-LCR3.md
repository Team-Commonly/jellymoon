# LCR3: CodeRabbit silence for 30 minutes no longer blocks a land

**Changed.** Owner ruling: land needs the PR's one local review, green CI and one CodeRabbit ask at the final head; if CodeRabbit
has not reviewed that head within 30 minutes of the ask, land proceeds. Land now has a third way to meet check (c): a trusted
ask (an issue comment by a `User` in `JM_TRUSTED_LOGINS`, body exactly `@coderabbitai review` or `@coderabbitai full review`,
created after the head became the PR's head), and at least `FALLBACK_MS` (30 minutes, one constant) between the earliest such ask
and land's own clock. Only "CodeRabbit has not reviewed this head" can be waited out: no summary, a rate limited or skipped
summary, or a well-formed summary of an older head. A `CHANGES_REQUESTED` review, a not-clean summary at the head, ambiguous or
steered comments and unreadable replies still refuse, as do (a), (b), (d) and (e) beside any ask. The override table is in
`docs/development/landing.md`, and the policy is one paragraph in `docs/development/workflow.md`.

**Head time.** Taken from the PR timeline (`GET issues/{n}/timeline`, paginated, read only when a trusted ask exists): the latest
`committed` (committer date) or `head_ref_force_pushed` (GitHub's time) event for the head sha. Preferred over the commit's own
date because a rebase or amend rewrites it and the timeline ties it to this PR. Limit: the timeline has no server time for an
ordinary push, and a committer date is set by the committer, so a backdated commit could let an ask for an older head count.

**Evidence.** The red case (ask 31 minutes old, no review) was committed first. Refuse cases and the e2e routes are in
`scripts/check-land-coderabbit.mjs` and `scripts/check-land-e2e.mjs`. Six mutants are each caught. Read-only run on live data:
PR 16 passes via the fallback, PR 18 (never asked) refuses for no ask.
