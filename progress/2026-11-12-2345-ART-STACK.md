# The seven findings of #145's after-merge review

One PR, branch `art-stack`. The art door is not what leaves the machine or what an agent may do on it, so it lands
on green CI and is reviewed after.

## The principle

Sharing the renderer's parser ended the differences between the door and the renderer. It did not make the parser
safe to hand a file nobody has judged: a path of nothing but `z` ended the program inside the parse, in the door
and the renderer alike. So what a value makes the parser do is held before the parse, on the raw text, with the
parser library's own tokenizer (ADR 0015, "What the parser does by the length of a value"). And a count is only a
bound on what was measured: a fill is now counted by the rows its edges cross, since that is what the rasteriser
pays, and the README says what the heaviest themes are a list of.

Deleted: the heaviest art that spent its points on stroke-only lines and its curved twin

budget is raised by 607 and xtask's by 37, each with its reason in `entropy.toml`.

## How each finding is held

1. **A path of repeated closes overflowed the stack.** `theme/svg_text.rs` walks every `d` with
   `svgtypes::PathParser` and every `points` with `PointsParser` before `Tree::from_xmltree`. A path holds at most
   32 closes or arcs in a row that the parser may draw nothing for (`MAX_ART_IDLE`), a file at most 2048 commands
   (`MAX_ART_COMMANDS`), no written number is over 1000000 (`MAX_ART_WRITTEN`), and an arc's two radii are
   within 1000 times of each other (`MAX_ART_FLAT`): the first whole run of the tests found a flat arc between
   far ends made of 81 curves where the size alone was thought to hold it to 16.
   `tests/limits.rs::a_run_of_segments_the_parser_draws_nothing_for_is_held_to_a_length` is the table of every
   segment against the parser's own count, and pins the review's file.
   `tests/art_draw.rs::the_worst_files_the_door_takes_are_read_and_drawn_on_a_small_stack` reads and rasterises
   the longest runs, the most commands and the deepest nesting on a thread of 256 KiB, in a debug build.
2. **A filled path was underpriced.** Each fill and stroke costs the picture's area and 16 (`ROW_PAINT`) for every
   row an edge crosses, twice for a stroke, all of it four times under a gradient. The review's picture is 19
   times a file's paint; it and its three neighbours are refused
   (`tests/cost.rs::a_fill_and_a_stroke_are_counted_by_the_rows_their_edges_cross`). `tests/draw.rs` spends both
   counts to the last unit five ways, the review's shape among them.
3. **Reach under a slant.** A stroke reaches the path's box after every transform and twice its drawn width
   (`tests/cost.rs::a_shape_stays_within_reach_of_its_picture_with_its_stroke`, with the review's matrix).
4. **A size that rounds to no pixel.** A side is at least 0.5 (`MIN_ART_SIDE`);
   `tests/art_draw.rs::a_picture_under_half_a_pixel_a_side_is_refused_as_the_renderer_refuses_it` holds the door
   and the renderer together on both sides of it.
5. **The smoke chain's target folder** is the one `cargo metadata` names
   (`build.rs::the_target_folder_is_the_one_cargo_names_whatever_moved_it`, with a cargo home of its own).
6. **Six mutations that survived.** Each has a test, and each was run against its mutation and failed: reach past
   the bottom alone and a stroke's reach without its mitre (`tests/cost.rs`), a prefixed attribute
   (`tests/limits.rs`), the stroke floor at 0.5 (`tests/cost.rs`), the tree's arm for an image and the options
   that load none (inside `svg_check.rs`), `-p` with its value (`build.rs`). A seventh found on the way, an arc
   after a close counted in the run, has its edge in `tests/limits.rs`.
7. **Docs.** `tests/alike.rs` names `tests/art_draw.rs`. The nesting that overflows is stated once, with its stack.

## What was read (item 1)

`svgtypes` 0.16.2, `usvg` 0.46.0's conversion and `kurbo` 0.13.1's arcs, for everything done by the length or size
of a value. The table is in ADR 0015. In short: the simplifying path parser calls itself for a close after a close
and for an arc of no curve; an arc, a circle, an ellipse and a rounded corner are made of more curves the larger
they are written, all at once; the curves are taken from the front of a list; the stroker runs while the tree is
built; nesting recurses a level at a time; gradient stops are compared pairwise. Everything else read is a plain
loop over the value. Not read: `roxmltree`, the text modules, and whether the stroker always ends on the largest
numbers the door lets through.

## Measured (one Mac; a debug build unless a release is named)

- The five heaviest themes, rasterised: 1.30 s (52 covering fills), 1.10 s (a ragged fill), 0.64 s (a ragged
  gradient), 0.77 s (a ragged fill under a round stroke), 0.84 s (ragged curves under the thinnest stroke). In a
  release: 56, 80, 24, 35 and 32 ms. Read at the door in 3 to 16 ms, and under 1 ms in a release.
- The review's picture, refused now: 7.6 s (fill 3.0 s, stroke 4.6 s); 14.1 s and 461 ms in a release in the review.
- One ragged fill of 1535 points: 0.78 s opaque, 1.38 s under a see-through gradient; the same points along the
  rows 30 ms. The time followed the points: 0.48 s at 768, 0.27 s at 384.
- Stack, the parse on a thread of its own: a run of closes overflows 256 KiB at 96 (80 is read) and 128 KiB at 48
  (32 is read). Nesting overflows 256 KiB at 80 deep (60 is read) and 2 MiB at 700 (600 is read); at 1024 deep the
  parser refuses the file itself, which a stack of 4 MiB reaches.
- A circle of radius 1e6 is 49 points, 1e12 457, 1e18 4489. An arc of radii 1e6 and 1 between ends 2.8e6 apart
  is 81 curves; with the radii within 1000 times, the most seen is under 65.
- The kit's own art, counted the new way: 2260 numbers and 2551696 paint, 74% and 61% of the counts.

## Not done, and why

- **An arc is counted in the run whether it is flat or not.** Telling them apart is an arc's arithmetic, which
  would be a second reading of a path. So a path of more than 32 arcs in a row with nothing between them is
  refused though it may be honest. A ruling could raise 32: 80 is read on 256 KiB.
- **64 curves an arc is the most measured, not a proof.** It is five arcs at the edges of the two limits
  (`tests/limits.rs`), and 2048 commands of them are held by the count of numbers and paint after the parse.
- **The five heaviest are not a proof.** They are the dearest of what the kit and two reviews measured. A mix of
  covering fills and ragged edges was not timed.
- **See-through paint that covers the picture is still the dearest unit in a debug build**, 1.3 s for a theme, and
  is counted as it was.
- **A gradient is counted four times a colour on rows too**, where it measured about two and a half times a fill
  and one and a half times again for a stroke. It errs dear.
- A `build.target` in a cargo config still puts the programs where the chain does not look.
- Three of `tests/draw.rs`'s tests fail in a release build (they read what GPUI keeps only in a debug one). They
  did before this change; the release times above are from the heaviest test alone.
- The reds were seen as the tests were written, not as a separate commit.
