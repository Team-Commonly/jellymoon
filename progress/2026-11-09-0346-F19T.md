# F19T: tests for the two gaps the connector-hardening review left

**Changed.** Tests only; no test showed a bug, so no source changed. Three tests in `server/test/connector-hardening.test.ts`:
- *The claim is part of the ruling* (two cases). A Db wrapper makes the claim of the parked row throw once, or rolls back the
  ruling's transaction after its work, claim included. Either way the ruling is refused, the decision stays `open`, the receipt
  reads `parked` and the upstream is called 0 times. Two approve rulings then race through the pod: one is accepted, one is
  refused, the decision is `ruled` and the upstream count is exactly 1, with the answer on the receipt.
- *A late answer to a write settled as unknown.* The release's upstream call hangs; the clock moves past the 60 s answer budget,
  and the receipts read settles the row as `unknown`. Then the original call answers: the receipt becomes `released`, 201, with
  its result and no reason. A restart (`Hub.restore`) after that sends nothing, and the upstream count stays 1.

**Evidence.** Each mutant was applied, run and reverted, leaving a clean tree:
- the claim moved beside the upstream call, after the ruling commits, on the hub's Db: the claim-fails case is red (the ruling
  is accepted, and the write is never claimed or sent);
- the claim kept in the ruling's step but on the hub's Db instead of the transaction: PGlite's single connection deadlocks, so
  five tests time out, these among them;
- the late answer may replace only `released`: the late-answer test is red (it stays `unknown`);
- the release calls the upstream again when its row reads `unknown`: the late-answer test is red (count 2).

The rollback case does not catch the first mutant (it claims nothing before the rollback, so the end state is the same). It
guards a claim made outside the transaction but before its commit, on a store that does not serialise on one connection.
