# F63: a day nothing was typed on has a page of its made wishes and an entry

**What changed.** On branch `f63-quiet-day`, on main `275688a`. A day with no session logs at all had no page:
the reader's answer is an error. Core's `page_with_no_logs` (`crates/jellymoon-core/src/no_logs.rs`) now gives
that day a page when it has a made wish or a project on offer: the empty page every day of no turns has, made
with no call, with `read.no_logs` true. `jellymoon page` prints it, and the app keeps and draws it. The entry's
chips are every name core offers (`jellymoon_core::offered`), whatever the page read, so a listed project has a
chip on a day nothing was typed in it. The kit draws the first six options and counts the rest, "+94 more"
(`PieceKind::More`). What was decided is ADR 0020, "Found while building F63". F63 is `passes: true`.

**The evidence.** `cargo test -p jellymoon-app --test f63` with the two headless features (8 tests),
`cargo test -p jellymoon --test f63` (6 tests), the kit's `tests/entry_layout.rs`, the example
`page-no-logs.json`, which round-trips, and the smoke chain's `project` step, which now asks `jellymoon page`
over a logs folder that is not there. The app's test was seen red first: six of seven failed for a missing
entry and chip. The binary's test was written before core's rule and first run after it, so it was not seen
red; 20 one-rule mutations stand in for that, each seen to fail a test (one lived at first, and got one).

Two earlier tests changed their answer. `app/tests/f37.rs`: a made wish over logs that are not there was
"Today could not be read."; it is the day's page now, and its footer says no logs were found.
`app/tests/f42/notes.rs`: the chips were the kept page's projects that are on offer, cut to six by the app;
they are every name on offer, in core's order, and the kit draws six.

**What's next.** F64 (a folder dropped on the window) and F66 ("Start something new" in the entry) stand on this.

**Surprising.**
- Only the day with no logs needed a page. Logs that are there and hold no turn of the day already printed an
  empty page with no call and the made wishes on it; that day is pinned now and prints the bytes it did.
- The app offered a chip only for a project of the page's reading, so a listed project had none on any day.
- The folder the smoke chain calls `no-logs` is there and holds nothing. The `project` step names another
  for its page.
- `jellymoon page` decorates the "no session logs" line only for the folder nobody named, so a test of that
  line names Claude Code's own folder in its world.

**Open.**
- A day whose logs hold no turn still has the footer "Read from 0 things you typed. This is the model's
  reading, unedited.", though no model read it. Changing it changes a page that is drawn today.
- A made wish's question is not drawn on a page, on this day or any. It is proved at `jellymoon page`.
- Which six of many projects have a chip is core's order: typed in that day first, then the list's. A project
  past the sixth is wished for with `jellymoon wish`.
- The app's refusal of a wish for a project not on offer still says "typed in" (open since F62).
- `wishable` is still public in core and the app no longer calls it; core's `wish_offered` does.
- Not run: Windows and Linux, `make preflight`, and a person's walk at a window.
