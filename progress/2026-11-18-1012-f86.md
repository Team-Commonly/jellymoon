# F86: what a run says it did, over the choice to keep it or toss it

## Built
- The kit gains `Note::Told` (`crates/sticker-kit/src/scene.rs`, `layout/told.rs`): a title, rows of a label in
  the note's own words over someone else's texts, each a `Quote` piece in `Role::Quoted` and never a run of the
  sheet, and the note's own sentences last. Rows are drawn whole, in order, up to the first that does not fit;
  it and every row after it are counted (`+N more`), and no later row takes its place. A text cleaning would
  cut is a row that does not fit. A row `with_last` is one unit with the row before it (a change's before and
  after), drawn together or not at all. A row with an empty label starts with its quote. No option, field or mark.
- The coordinator's ruling: a told note is never planned at fewer rows than fit, so a plan that quotes more of
  it wins over one that places more notes (`Plan::told`).
- Placement (`layout/notes.rs`): a told note is planned just after the note that follows it in the scene, with
  the rows that fit the room that note leaves (at least its title, count and own sentences), so the work's note
  is placed and drawn as it is without a result and the notes after them give way and are counted; the told
  note is drawn in its own place, over the work's note. A first try planned it at its least and the notes after
  took the column (a told note with no row on the test day at 1440x900); that was the third hold's red. The work's note
  is then lower, every piece the same relative to its top, but for the sheet's and tape's `index` (its place in
  the scene).
- The app (`app/src/told.rs`, `working.rs`): `told-<wish>` before `work-<wish>` in `layout`, when a wish is
  opened, and with `Show me`'s file; it goes with the work's note on a keep, a toss or the ask once more. Title
  "What it says it did", which is the did text's label (no `Did` label); labels `Changed in <name>, before`,
  `Changed in <name>, after` (one unit), `New file <name>`,
  `Decided`, `Not sure about`; last "Nothing was run to try this. A run reads and edits files, and no more." and,
  with a dropped piece, "Something the run said could not be checked, so it is not here."
- `app/examples/snapshot.rs` takes `--work <waiting file> [--told <shown file>]`.

## Red first
`app/tests/f86.rs` (5 tests) and `crates/sticker-kit/tests/told.rs` (5) all failed against a stub `Note::Told`
that the layout left out. Under the coordinator's later ruling (order kept, a change one unit, no `Did` label),
the kit's order-and-unit test and the app's 960x640 test were added and the others changed; the 960x640 check
was red against the old rule (a later, shorter row drawn in place of the did) before the rule changed.
