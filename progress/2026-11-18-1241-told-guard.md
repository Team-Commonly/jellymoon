# Told guard: a result never costs a later note its choices

## Built
- `told::kept` (`crates/sticker-kit/src/layout/told.rs`) refuses the layout with the told notes when a note with an
  option or a field (`told::chooses`: a choice with options, a text with options or a field) is drawn without the told
  notes and left out with them. A note with nothing to choose may still give way and be counted, as before.
- The fallback is the guard's own: the layout without the told notes is kept, so the result gives way, never the
  choices. A person sees both work notes with all their choices and no result note; where the count line fits, the
  result is counted in it ("+1 more of 3 notes").
- README: one sentence where the guard is described. `entropy.toml`: the kit's budget 11027 -> 11085.

## Red first
- `tests/told.rs` `a_result_never_costs_a_later_note_its_choices` (960x640, long result for A, A's work, then B's work
  with three options or a clash of three): B's options 0, expected 3.
- `tests/told_sweep.rs`: a third case after the work's note (a second work's note with three choices) and the rule
  asserted on every scene (the ids drawn with an option, an offer or a field match the layout without a result).
  Red at the first scene, 960x640, one line, a typical result: `["work-1"]` against `["work-1", "work-2"]`.
- Two older tests in `tests/told.rs` put choice notes after the told note to show notes giving way; under the new rule
  those are notes with nothing to choose (`plain`).
