# F28 · the app draws a page from page data using only the kit (2026-11-10)

**What changed.** `crates/sticker-kit` and `app/` (`jellymoon-app`) came onto `main` as files, each with a door. The
app refuses a page file over 1 MiB, draws the first 24 wishes, 8 clashes, 12 stars a wish and 8 unknown items, and
counts the rest. The kit holds every theme, from a file or built in code, to one range check, counts the notes that
do not fit, and takes theme art only as a flat picture: 15 elements, 33 attributes, 100 elements, 12 deep, 256 a side.

**Evidence.** `make preflight` green on a Mac without Xcode's `metal`: 150 tests. F28 at the binary: 23 passed, run
with `--features runtime_shaders`. The heaviest art the door lets through draws in 7 s in a debug build.
