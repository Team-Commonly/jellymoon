# The cleaning is main's again, and the fault is fixed at the door (PR 128)

- **Put back.** `redact.rs` and the cleaning's part of `reader.md` are `origin/main`'s, byte for byte;
  `redact/twice.rs` and `redact/planted.rs` are gone. Two gates failed on changes to the cleaning, each opening a
  new hole, so this PR no longer touches it. Its own improvements live on the branch `redact-hardening`.
- **The door.** `still.rs` in core: a statement and a made wish are cleaned again, at most 8 times, until a cleaning
  changes nothing, and cut only then. Still changing after 8, or over 4096 bytes: a wish is refused in one line, a
  statement is `[redacted]`. What is kept is unchanged by one more cleaning, which is what reading it back does.
  A session log's turn is cleaned once, as before.
- **Files from before.** Words in a wishes file, a settlement or a waiting ruling are read through the same door:
  drawn as they stand still, and written so by the next wish. A day is not lost to them.
- **Evidence.** `tests/f37/still.rs` and `tests/f35/still.rs` failed first on main's behaviour, before the door was
  written; their texts are `support::restless`. Core: 26,112 built texts, each unchanged by one more making.
- **Surprises.** A text cut before it stands still keeps half of a key not yet found, so the cut comes last. That
  made twelve keys in a row a real text that outlasts 8 cleanings. A wish's 200 are counted at the end.
