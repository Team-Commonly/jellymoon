**Decided.**
- The filter stays in `first`, the door every caller goes through (`main.rs`, `last`, the tests): what is drawn
  first and what is handed on are decided together there. `count` and `without_logs` take what they are handed
  as an earlier page. `later` is public, so a caller that hands it a no-logs page of its own gets that page
  treated as an earlier one of 0 turns: replaced by core's page on a day with no logs, kept when the count is
  the same.
- The kept file is still read twice on a day with no logs: once in `first`, once in `without_logs` to see that
  core's page is the one kept. Reading it once needs `first` to hand the page on beside the scene, which
  changes its signature and so `main.rs` and the tests' own windows, files this change was told to leave. The
  second read happens only with no earlier page handed on.
- `Error::Call` has a body of its own, since the shared one named a reading.

**Open.**
- Core gives `Error::Call` for a wait that fails on a call already started (`call.rs`). The app says it as
  not started. The comment on `tried` and `app.md` say so; telling the two apart is core's to do.
- A call that took too long is held by a unit test only: the limit is three minutes and fixed.
- The old footer's words stay in `progress/2026-11-17-1450-APP-NOTES.md`, which is history.
