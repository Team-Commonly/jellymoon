# Entry keys: typing goes to the entry

## Built
- The kit (`crates/sticker-kit/src/draw/entry.rs`): a page with an entry gives the entry the keys whenever nothing
  else on the page holds them. Each frame checks first (the window opening, the first entry arriving, a field that
  went or was kept apart); a press off the fields and Escape give the entry the keys at once, not at the next frame.
  Escape in a note's field moves the keys to the entry; in the entry it keeps them there. A click on a field or its
  offer still gives that field the keys.
- The theme key: a bare `t` is always a letter. The theme switches on the command key and T (Ctrl and T off the
  Mac), which no field takes (`switches_theme(key, command)`). The keystroke observer moved from `shell::wire` into
  `window::view`, so the page's window on the test platform has it too.
- Docs: `docs/development/app.md` (Keys, the wait, the act table, what stays untested, the jellyfish's theme),
  the kit's README (Keys, a window's own keys). ADRs 0019 and 0022 still say `t`; they record past decisions.

## Red first
- `app/tests/entry_keys.rs`, five tests in the page's window on GPUI's test platform: typed with no click on a page
  of projects (`("", "", false)` against `("wish", "tidy the table", true)`), on a first page (`("new", "", false)`),
  after a tap on a character and on `Keep it`, after Escape, and the command key and T. All five red.
