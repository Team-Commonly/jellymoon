# FLAKE-TICK

Made `server/test/failure.test.ts` "a failed tick leaves the timer armed" deterministic.

- Cause: the test read `Date.now()` for the hub clock and polled with a wall-clock deadline, so host load decided the outcome.
- Fix: fake timers and a test-owned clock; the test waits on the hub's next `rearm` (the end of each tick), never on time. Moved into its own `describe` to stay under the function-length budget. `server/test/bun-jest.d.ts` declares the fake-clock calls the shared bun:test stub lacks.
- No server/src change: `Hub.now` and `Hub.rearm` were already the seam.
- Evidence, 20 runs of the one test at natural host load 30 to 48: before 9/20 failed, after 0/20. Mutant (no re-arm after a failed tick) fails the test.
