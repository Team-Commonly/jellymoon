# F86 gate fix: a result costs no other note anything, and a file's name is never a line of Jellymoon's

## Blocker 1: a result took Show me / Keep it / Toss it off its own work's note
- Causes found:
  1. `plan` kept room for the count under every note but the last, and the told note counted in the total, so
     the work's note was measured short of its room and fell back to its least form.
  2. A text note's body lines are a floor of its room (`text_note.rs`, `fits`), and the room at draw time is a sum
     of floats measured from another top than the plan's: at an exact fit it came out a hair short, a line was
     lost, and below two lines the note was not drawn at all (the 17-line note with a field and a long option at
     960x640, the second hold's red).
  3. A text note planned at its least form is drawn whole when the room allows, so a plan-level check of heights
     cannot see what a told note costs it.
- Now:
  - Room for the count is kept only on account of a note that is not a told note (`told::reserved`).
  - A long note is drawn in no less than its planned height plus half a pixel, so an exact fit stays the fit
    it was planned at; a told note that is not drawn takes nothing after it with it.
  - By construction (`told::guarded`): with a told note in the scene, the notes are laid out twice, with and
    without the told notes. Where a note other than a told note or a whole list is drawn in both and differs but
    for its place down the page, or the note a drawn told note is over is drawn only without them, the layout
    without the told notes is the one kept. A later note may give way whole, left out and counted. The cost is
    a second layout, only when a told note is in the scene.
- `tests/told_sweep.rs`: body lines 1 to 24, with and without the follow-up field, the `Whole` list and a long
  option, both themes, 960x640, 1440x900, 960 wide at 705 to 848 (step 13) and 960x800, with the typical result
  and a long one: 11520 scenes, the work's note's pieces equal those with no told note; the told note is drawn
  in 4272 of them. 48 seconds in a debug build.
- `tests/told.rs`: the two tests that put a told note over a work's note with notes after it now use notes of
  their own ids that keep their height (choices), so the ones that give way are left out whole.

## Blocker 2: a file's name in the label's voice
- `ToldRow.name`: a name shares the label's line only when the label and it lay out as one line and it holds no
  blank, unseen or control character (the kit's `is_blank`, `is_unseen`); else the label stands alone ("Changed
  in", "New file") and the name is the row's first quote, drawn whole or left out with its row.
- The typical result keeps "Changed in src/shipping.rs" on one line, so its room is as before.
- `tests/told.rs`: names ending in " Decided", holding a sentence, of 198 characters, with a zero-width space,
  at both sizes and themes: no label line holds a word of the name unless it is that one line.

## Notes
- ADR 0023 has "Found while building F86": the words are the app's, and what is said above. `working.rs`'s
  header says so. `PieceKind::More` documents the plain count line. `docs/development/app.md` names
  `shown-told-typical.json` and says a result takes room from later works' notes, never its own.
