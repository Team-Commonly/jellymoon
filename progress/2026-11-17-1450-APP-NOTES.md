# App notes: the seven notes of the review after the F63 notes, and one sentence on `--bare`

**What changed.** On branch `app-notes`, on `6c2f67f` (the F57 notes). One commit; no row of
`feature_list.json` changes, and core's code is untouched.

- **The keep rule is asked one way** (`app/src/today.rs`). The page of a day with no logs replaces a kept page
  only when that page holds nothing a reading gives (`read_nothing`). A kept page of 0 turns that holds a wish,
  a clash or a project read is an earlier reading, and stays under its note.
- **The footer says what was read**: "Nothing typed on this day was read, so no model read it." Turns typed in
  a folder switched off or in a run's copy are left out, and a made wish was typed in the window, so the old
  words could print false.
- **Two titles for a day with no page.** "There is no page for this day yet." where no reading call was made
  (no logs, logs or a `skip.txt` that could not be read, a `--model` that is no name, no Claude Code). "Today
  could not be read." only where a call was made and gave no page (`tried` in `today.rs`).
- **A kept page that says no logs were found is not drawn first.** The waiting scene is, over the day's made
  wishes; the count step then draws the kept page when there are still no logs, and does not write it again.
- **Docs**: `app.md` (what is shown first, the day with no logs, the two titles, "that read something", one
  line of 181 characters wrapped), `page.md` (`read.turns` 0 says what was read), ADR 0020's sentence of the
  footer, a comment naming the layout's `LEAST` in the kit's `tests/entry_layout.rs`.
- **`--bare`** (`page.md`, `run.md`): Claude Code's headless page says `--bare` "will become the default for
  `-p` in a future release" and that bare mode does not use the subscription sign-in. The reading call and a
  run are `-p` calls that rest on that sign-in. Written down as open; nothing is built.

**The evidence.** Red first on `6c2f67f`: 7 tests of `tests/f63.rs`, 2 of `tests/f34.rs`, 1 of `tests/f28.rs`
and 1 unit test of `scene.rs`. 15 one-rule mutations; 12 were killed at `layout` and through `Today`, and the
three that survived there (a call that took too long, one that could not be started, a harness's own error
are each a tried reading) are held by a unit test of `tried`, since the made-up harness plays none of them
at the app.

**Decided.**
- Which title is said goes by the error, not by the step: a `--model` refused and a missing Claude Code reach
  the call's step and no call is made, so they are "no page yet".
- The first scene of a kept no-logs page hands on no page, so the window has no entry until the count step
  gives one: a moment, where the stale footer showed before.
- With the waiting scene first, a no-logs page that is the same as the kept one is drawn by the count step
  where it gave nothing before.

**Surprising.**
- "There is no page for this day yet." is two lines in a note at the designed size, where the old title was
  one. `tests/f34.rs` allowed one title line only.
- The old footer's words stay in `progress/2026-11-15-0909-F63-NOTES.md`, which is history. Core's comment on
  `read.no_logs` (`shapes.rs`) and ADR 0020's decision text still say that 0 turns means nothing was typed:
  core is out of this change's scope.

**What's next.** Nothing of this change. The page does not move to a new day at midnight (open, `app.md`).
