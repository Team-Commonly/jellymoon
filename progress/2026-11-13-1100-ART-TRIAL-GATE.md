# The gate on PR 154: the child's own clock, the page's stack, a stated cost

Same PR, branch `art-trial`, one commit on `98aaf0d`. The gate failed it with four blocking findings and six
others. ADR 0016 is amended; `crates/sticker-kit/README.md` has the measurements.

## What changed

1. **The child has a clock of its own** (`theme/stand.rs`), started before it reads a byte. It ends the process
   with code 124 at the limit, which the caller gives as the last argument and which is never more than
   `TRIAL_LIMIT`. An orphan lives at most the limit. The caller still ends and waits for its child.
2. **A percent from 0 to 100 is taken wherever a length is placed** (`theme/svg_text.rs`), and one outside is
   refused by name. A comment that ends before the next `<` is not counted as attributes. `tests/ordinary.rs` is
   the standing set: ten ordinary pictures, each accepted through the real child with four times room under each
   cost. Every later change to a rule or a cost must accept all of them.
3. **The child reads and draws on a stack of 512 KiB** (`PAGE_STACK`), the smallest any system gives the thread
   that draws a page's pictures (macOS 512 KiB, Windows 1 MiB, Linux 2 MiB). The idle-arc rule holds how far from
   its own place the parser may stand, so 1022 arcs written to one place are a run of 1022 (`tests/idle.rs`).
4. **A picture is accepted only under a stated cost** (`theme/meter.rs`): 100 ms of processor time in a release
   and 2 s in a debug build, and 32 MiB of memory over what the child held before. What is over is
   `refused: drawing it took more than …`, said as soon as it is passed, and kept. A child that cannot measure
   refuses. `TRIAL_LIMIT` is 4 s in a release now: the clock is for what the meter does not see.

And: only an answer about the picture is kept (a trial that ended badly, ran over or was unsure is tried again);
the key carries the build and the caller's stamp; the kept folder is 0700 and its files 0600 on Unix, and a link in
the folder's or a file's place is not followed (`theme/kept.rs`); the window's use of the tried art has a test
(`app/tests/f32/window.rs`); test folders are under `CARGO_TARGET_TMPDIR`.

New dependency lines, noted in ADR 0016: `sysinfo` 0.31.4 (`system` only, already in `Cargo.lock`), and `rustix`
for every Unix with `time`.

Lines: 1584 added and 327 removed in 32 files; Rust is 1307 and 257. Deleted: the rule that only the picture's
own `width` and `height` may be a percent, the keeping of a refusal that says nothing of the picture, and the
kept answers' code in `trial.rs`, which moved to `kept.rs`. Budgets: the kit 10082 to 11075, the app 5624 to 5681.

## Proof

Each new test was seen red on `98aaf0d`'s behaviour, except the ones marked.

- `tests/trial_clock.rs`: a child nobody watches ends itself at its limit; a child whose caller was ended
  mid-trial is gone within its limit; a child that floods or does not read holds its caller no longer than the
  limit (not red: it holds mutations M11 and, on Windows, M12); a child reads no more than a picture may be (not
  red: M18); one trial at a time (not red: M25. The helper let its lock go at once, so the test only caught a
  dropped lock by chance; it holds it now).
- `tests/trial_kept.rs`: only an answer about the picture is kept; a kept answer is for the caller that asked;
  the kept folder is the person's alone.
- `tests/ordinary.rs`: the standing set; 60 and 100 pairs of curves refused for cost, in about 2 s each in a
  debug build, and the refusal kept; 1022 arcs to one place refused by rule, and held to no rule they cost one
  child and the caller lives.
- `tests/idle.rs`, `tests/limits.rs`: the arcs, the percents, the comment.
- `theme/meter.rs`: each cost over its most is refused by name.
- `app/tests/f32.rs` and `f32/window.rs`: the app's key and lines; the window is given the art its trials
  accepted, and only for the theme on show.

Mutations, one at a time, 28: the gate's M01, M10, M11, M18, M21, M22, M25 and M28 are caught, M01 by tests that
fail within their own clocks (98 s and 36 s) and leave nothing running. Alive:

- M12, the picture written on the caller's thread, on macOS and Linux: a pipe there holds 64 KiB, the largest
  picture. The test holds it on Windows, where a pipe holds 4 KiB; CI is the first to run that.
- The stack `stand.rs` draws on made large. The test of the small stack draws through the helper's own thread
  of `PAGE_STACK`; no picture inside the rules is known to need more than 512 KiB.
- The kept folder made 0755: it is set to 0700 the moment after, before anything is read or kept.
- A cost found over only once the draw had ended being said as unsure: the meter's own thread says it first.

## Not closed

- A peak of memory between two asks of the meter (2 ms) is not seen.
- Processor time on Windows is time on the wall: a refusal for it there is not kept.
- An orphan goes on to its limit, 4 s at most in a release; it does not end when its caller's pipe closes.
- The idle-arc rule counts some arcs the parser would draw: it is the safe side, and not exact.
- The kept folder between the look and the use.
- `app/tests/support/wished.rs` still makes its folder with `tempfile`.
- Three text tests of `tests/draw.rs` fail in a release test build. They draw no art; not looked into.
- Linux and Windows were not run here: the Linux limit, the meter on both, folder modes on Linux, the non-Unix
  halves of `kept.rs` and `meter.rs`.
