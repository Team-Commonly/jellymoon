# Work that waits shows itself (work shows)

## Built
- `app/src/showing.rs`: `Showing::look` asks core of a wish whose run ended and puts its work on the page, in the
  look that draws the star. `Turns` (on the window's thread) keeps whose turn it is: the made wishes this window
  saw being worked on or saw made, less the ones shown and the ones core said have no work waiting. The look's
  own thread tells the last through a shared list. `Showing::seen` takes shown work off the turns.
- `app/src/working.rs`: `in_hand` (a note a person may be about to tap or type in), `shown` (the wishes whose work
  is on a scene), `Today::waiting_over` (a tap's notes of work that waits, over a scene). `notes_on_open` and
  `opened` now share `work_notes` and `over` with it; a tap gives what it gave.
- The made-up window (`tests/support/tapper.rs`) has a `look` act.
- `docs/development/app.md`: "Work that waits shows itself", the look, the walk's command line.

## Decided
- **Work that waited when the window opened**: the first look that is free to shows the newest done wish's work
  that waits (the done wishes are asked from the newest until one waits). Once a window, one work.
- **On show** is the scene on show, placed or not: a choice with an option, a text note with an option or a
  field, `busy-<wish>`, "That run is being stopped.", and a wish's note that says it is being worked on.
- **Several ends**: the first in core's order; the others wait behind its choices.
- **What was said of work kept or tossed stays** under work a look opens (a tap on a character takes it away).
  Without this the answer to `Keep it` was gone within a second when another wish's work waited its turn.
