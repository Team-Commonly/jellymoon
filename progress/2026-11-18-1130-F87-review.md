# F87: what a light review found

## Built
- A run that asks with a `result` beside its question asks: `run/asks.rs`, `last()`, reads it as `Asks`, the
  question held to the same checks as before, and no result is kept or printed (`Word::result` reads one beside
  `done` only). Before, the schema of F87 let a harness write that word and core failed the run.
- The fixed words name each kind's parts (`did`, `decided`, `unsure`: only `text`; `change`: only `file`,
  `before`, `after`), since one piece with a wrong part loses the whole result (`told.rs` reads pieces with
  `deny_unknown_fields`, untouched). They say `file` is relative with no leading `./`, and that `decided` and
  `unsure` are under 160 characters.
- Core's told test asserts the counts and lengths the words quote (`BEFORE`, `ORDER`, `LINES`, `LINE_MAX`,
  `SAYS_MAX`).
- `run.md`: which rule of a result leads to which outcome (no result, a piece dropped and counted, a piece left
  out and not counted), checked against `told.rs`; `asks` with a result; a line for the third measure.
- `entropy.toml`: a comment line that stood twice is gone; budgets set to the lines as they are.
