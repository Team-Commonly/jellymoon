# F54 notes: the blocker and the notes of the review of what is typed under a wish

**What changed.** On branch `f54-notes`, on `6c2f67f`. One commit; no row of `feature_list.json` changes, and
nothing of core's code.

- **Words typed under `More like this` survive a tap on a choice.** The field is the wish's, not the note's: the
  kit's `Field` has a `key` (`more-<wish>` here), and two notes that carry the same key and offer have one
  field. The note that says what is being done carries the same field, so the words are on show while core is
  asked, and Enter under it is a follow-up as under the work. A note that asks once more has no field; the kit
  keeps a keyed field that holds words apart from the scene, eight at most, until a note has it again.
- **An `Err` from `answer_run` is looked at again.** Core's error says why, not whether the run got the answer.
  The app asks core before and after the call whether the wish's run waits on the note's question. Still
  waiting, or never waiting: "That answer was not taken.", and the note is back with its field and its words.
  Waited and waits no more: "The answer was given, but what came after it failed.", the field is emptied, and
  the wish's note is as core says the wish stands.
- **From Enter of an answer the wish's note says "This wish is being worked on."**, core's words, with no
  question and no field, until core has answered. No key is held for the run's length. The field waits apart
  from the scene, so a refusal leaves its words when the note has its field again.
- **A field's words go when its offer changes**, so an answer is never left to be sent as a follow-up.
- **Only a made wish is drawn with a question and a field.** A question a kept page file gives a wish of the
  reading is not drawn.
- **Quit, not a closed window, cuts an answered run off**: said so in `answering.rs`, `app.md` and ADR 0018.
- **The window's own path has tests** on GPUI's test platform (`app/tests/f54/window.rs`): Enter in a field
  through `window::entered`, and an answer that comes after the window was closed.
- **Deslop**: `with_question` and `with_more` in place of the free `asking` and `offering` (`Today::asking` is
  another thing); one `Today::follows` in place of three copies; `with_more` says core never gives one wish both
  a question and a follow-up.

**The evidence.** Red first, against the old code with only the new names added: four of the kit's tests in
`tests/field_draw.rs` and seven of the app's in `tests/f54` (the busy note's field, the at-once note in two
tests, the answer given before core failed, the kept page file's question, and both window tests). 20 one-rule
mutations, 19 killed.

**What's next.** Nothing of this change. Open, as before: an answer started apart from the app's process, so
that Quit would not cut the run off.

**Surprising.**
- After an answer was given and the day was busy, the wish's note is its work that waits, not a wish at work:
  core's `waits` reads the run's record, which has the end the page of the day does not have yet.
- One mutation survives: the window giving core no words at all. The day of `tests/f54/window.rs` has no record
  of the run, so core refuses whatever was typed, and the page's view does not give its scene to a test. That
  the words reach core is held at the made-up window (`tests/support/tapper.rs`), not at the page's window.
- A note left on show with one question, while the run was answered elsewhere and now asks another, gives its
  words to the new question. Core has no way to say which question an answer is to. Written in `app.md`.
- Whether an answer was given is told by the question's text. A run that asks the very same words again after
  an answer that then failed reads as not given: the title is "That answer was not taken." and the words stay.
- The kit's tests of a key are on the Mac's test platform only, as its other draw tests are.
