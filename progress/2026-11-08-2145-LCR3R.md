# LCR3R: land never waits for CodeRabbit

**Changed.** The 30-minute ask fallback is gone, and with it the ask scan, the timeline read and the clock. A change needs one review from
any one source plus green CI. In `scripts/land-coderabbit.mjs`, check (c) now passes at once on a gap (no CodeRabbit review and no summary,
a rate limited or skipped summary, or a well-formed summary whose marker covers another commit), and the land output and `LANDED` note say
`no CodeRabbit review at this head` so these lands can be counted. A well-formed summary at the head passes whether or not its status line
is clean, because the one review already happened.

**Still refuses.** A `CHANGES_REQUESTED` review at the head, an unanswered Major or Critical thread, a missing earlier local review, a
`FAIL` at the head, an untrusted commit, a reviews or comments read that fails, and the forgery shapes (two or more summaries, a marker in
a reply, a malformed or repeated-key marker, a missing block, a marker of another kind).

**Docs.** `landing.md` and `workflow.md` state the rule in plain words. A Major or Critical raised after merge becomes a fix PR, first in
the queue.

**Evidence.** Red cases were committed first. Mutants and gates are in the builder report.
