# Art cost · what a theme's art costs to draw is counted at the door (2026-11-11)

**What changed.** The door counts two things in an art file (`theme::art_cost`): the numbers in its `d` and `points`
(at most 1024) and its paint, shapes x width x height, x 4 with a gradient (at most 64 shapes on 256 by 256). A
theme's files together are held to the same two, and a `viewBox` is at least 8 a side. One over is refused in one line.

**Evidence.** Debug build, a Mac: five files at the old limits (64 KiB each) took 38.1 s to draw, and 60.4 s on a
hosted runner, which failed the test. Five files that spend both counts to the last unit take 3.5 s, 1.4 s over the
page with no art. The kit's own art counts 368 numbers and 1728164 paint, 36% and 41% of the counts.

**What's next.** The draw tests keep a clock only as a backstop (40 s and 110 s, ten times measured).

**Surprising.** The old "heaviest" file was not the worst: its long paths lay off the picture and cost nothing. Time
follows pixels painted and lines walked per row, not bytes; a see-through stroke over a `viewBox` of 1 cost double.
