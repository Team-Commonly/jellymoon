# PLAN-HARNESS: living beside a person's own Claude Code (ADR 0021, F67 to F73)

**What changed.** On branch `plan-harness`, on main `3fa9354`. A plan only: no Rust. ADR 0021 writes down seven
decisions about being a guest beside the person's harness, each with what main does today and its file, and
`feature_list.json` gains F67 to F73, all `passes: false`. The glossary gains session store, shim and Jellymoon's log.

**The evidence.** `cargo fmt --all --check` and `cargo xtask check`, both clean. Every "today" was read in the code;
nothing was built, run or measured, and no test was run.

Both are gated, as are F69 to F72. F73 waits on F67.

  reader, the hook's exact removal and its write, and the stop on a Mac and on Linux.
