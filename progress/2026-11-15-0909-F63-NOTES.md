# F63 notes: the kept page of a day with no logs is no earlier reading

**What changed.** On branch `f63-notes`, on main `3fa9354`. Ten notes of the review after F63's merge.

- A kept page that says no logs were found (`read.no_logs`) is no earlier reading (`app/src/today.rs`). Once
  there are logs the day is read as for the first time, behind the waiting page; when it cannot be read the note
  is "Today could not be read.", over the day's made wishes.
- On a day with no logs, what core refuses the page for is the note, in core's words: a `--model` that is no
  name draws the model sentence `jellymoon page` prints, not the no-logs words.
- `read.no_logs` counts only on a page that holds nothing a reading gives: no turn, no project, no clash and no
  wish of its own (`read_nothing` in `app/src/scene.rs`). Core has no check of a whole page (its types are the
  check, and the app's door is `app/src/read.rs`), so the footer and the keep rule ask both, through that one
  function. A page that says no logs and holds a reading is drawn, and kept, as the reading.
- A day whose logs hold no turn of it has a footer of its own: "Nothing was typed on this day, so no model read
  it." It was "Read from 0 things you typed. This is the model's reading, unedited."
- `wishable` is `pub(crate)` in core. The kit works out the count of an entry's options only when there is a
  rest. One `quiet` in `app/tests/support/wished.rs`. Three lines over 120 wrapped.
- Docs: `page.md`, `app.md`, `smoke.md`, ADR 0020's "Found while building F63", and the parent named in
  `progress/2026-11-15-0450-F63-QUIET-DAY.md` (`275688a`).

**The evidence.** `app/tests/f63/kept.rs` (3 tests) and the footer's pins were seen red on main: 7 tests of
the app failed. Three tests stand for the review's surviving mutations and pass on main by their nature: made
wishes that cannot be used with no project on offer answer with the reader's line
(`crates/jellymoon/tests/f63.rs`); a field that holds three chips at their least width and the count, and not
six, has one count (`crates/sticker-kit/tests/entry_layout.rs`); the same page is not kept a second time, read
by the kept file's time (`app/tests/f63.rs`). 15 one-rule mutations, each seen to fail a test.

**Decided.**
- The comparison that keeps the same page from being written again stays; it has its test now.
- The footer asks both things of `read.no_logs`, since no check of a page lives in core to refuse the pair.
- The footer of a day with no logs stays fixed and names no folder: core's page does not carry it.

**Open.**
- The page of a day with no logs does not name the folder that was looked in. Naming it needs a field on
  core's page.
- A logs path that is a file, or that cannot be read, prints the page of a day with nothing typed (the
  review's note 11): the reader's, not changed here.
- `docs/development/project.md` has no "logs that are not there" to correct; `smoke.md` and the module doc of
  the `project` step had.
- Not run: Windows and Linux, `make preflight`, and a person's walk at a window.
