# F91: the wish is shaped before a run (2026-11-24)

## Built
Between Enter and a run, the person's own Claude Code is asked once, headless and read-only, to say the wish more
sharply (ADR 0025). Core: `run/shape.rs` (`jellymoon shape`, the fixed line and system words, the check, the one
shape that waits in `pages/<day>.shape.json`, `wish --shaped`, and the run's prompt with the person's own words
last). A made wish may hold `shaped`, and a run is given it. App: `src/shaping.rs`: the note that the wish is being
read, the sharper wish as quotes with two choices, and the tap that keeps the wish and starts its run.

Deleted: nothing.

## Proof
`crates/jellymoon/tests/f91.rs` (7 tests) and `app/tests/shape_first.rs` (5), with the made-up agent asked to
shape (`tests/support/fake_agent/shape.rs`). The core was written before these tests, so their red was shown by
six one-rule mutations of `run/shape.rs`, each killed by `f91.rs` and removed: an unknown field accepted, a cap not
applied, `is_error` accepted, a shape of other words used, a prompt without the person's words, a call started in
the project's folder. Two tests were red on their first run: a text with a line break is made one line by the door
and is no failure (the case was taken out), and the app's test took a note of the page's own for a refusal.
