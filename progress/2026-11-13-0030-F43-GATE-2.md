# F43, after the second gate: the six findings on `fbd98cf`

- **Done.** The one blocking and five other findings of the second gate on PR 143 are fixed in one commit on
  `f43`, each with a test seen red under its own mutation (nine mutations, one at a time, all killed). Docs:
  `docs/development/app.md` ("A wish in the window"), `docs/development/reader.md`, `crates/sticker-kit/README.md`.
- **1. An entry that goes away kept nothing.** The kit owns it (`Page::set_entry`, `draw/entry.rs`):
  `set_entry(None)` takes an empty entry off at once, and leaves one that holds words, or waits, on the page
  until it is empty. It then goes by itself: after an answer that kept the words, or when the person deletes
  them. The window (`window.rs::reads`) is unchanged and still gives the page each reading step's entry.
- **Decided: it stays with the chips it had.** The other choice was to draw it with none. With no chips the
  kit gives Enter no option, and the app answers "No project was chosen", which is not why the wish cannot be
  kept; the kit would also have to invent an entry its caller never gave, and the field would move under the
  person's words. With its chips nothing moves, Enter is asked of core like any other, and core refuses a
  project it no longer offers, in its own words. The chips can be stale; core is what decides.
- **2. An answer's identity.** No number was added. An entry that waits cannot go, so it cannot come back
  empty and take a second wish: `set_entry(None)` then `set_entry(Some)` leaves the first words waiting, the
  second words and their Enter are dropped, and one answer comes for one Enter
  (`an_answer_is_always_for_the_words_on_show…`).
- **3. A log that grows.** `session::turns` reads a log no further than its length when it was opened
  (`take(len)`). For a whole reading this changes one thing: what a session writes to a log after the reader
  opened it is not in that reading, where before it was if it landed before the reader's last read. A line half
  written at that length is cut off there, is not valid JSON and is skipped, as the unfinished last line of a
  log was before. A file that shrinks is read to its new end, as before. A file whose length cannot be asked
  gives no turns.
- **Its test** is `crates/jellymoon/tests/growing_log.rs`, at `jellymoon day`. A made-up session
  (`tests/support/fake_writer.rs`, a binary) writes 40,000 lines typed two days earlier, then a line of the day
  every millisecond; it ends itself at 20 seconds or 20 MB, and the test ends it before its folder goes. The
  test counts the day's lines before the command and after it, and asks that the day holds every turn that was
  there, in order, and no more than half of what was typed while it ran. It rests on time: the reader opens the
  log sooner after its start than it takes to read the 40,000 lines. Measured here, the old reader gave 1257 of
  1266 turns against 5 at the start. A wider gap would need a hook in the reader, which was not added.
- **4. Time zones.** Every made-up day of the app's tests is typed at noon of the day in the machine's zone
  (`support/day.rs::noon`), and the programs a test starts are given this process's `TZ` and not a fixed one
  (`zone`). `f34_kept.rs` did this for itself and no longer does. Core's own unit test of the lookup is typed
  the same way. `f42` (20) and `f43` (8) pass with `TZ=Asia/Tokyo`, `Pacific/Kiritimati` and `Pacific/Pago_Pago`.
- **5. Escape** is not taken while the entry waits: `entry_key` returns before any key. The keys stay the
  entry's, so the window's `t` does not switch the theme.
- **6. Three rules** each have a test: a page with no handler answers itself (`entry_draw.rs`); a folder counts
  only as a full path, and is matched only by its last name (`wish.rs`, one test, red under each mutation).
- **Lines.** 396 added, 57 removed, this file and the budgets' reasons among them. Deleted: the kept page's own noon stamping in `f34_kept.rs`, and the fixed
  `America/Los_Angeles` of the app's two test worlds. Budgets raised in `entropy.toml`, each with its reason.
- **Open.** `window.rs` is still run by no test: that the window keeps the words through a reading's end is
  proved in the kit and by reading. Not run on Windows or Linux here: CI's. The growing-log test's helper
  sleeps a millisecond a line, which Windows rounds up; the test's bound is a ratio, not a count.
