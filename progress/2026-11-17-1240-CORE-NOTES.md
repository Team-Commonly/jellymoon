# CORE-NOTES: a held lock is never removed by a caller that waits, five notes of F53's gate, logs that are no folder

**What changed.** On branch `core-notes`, on main `bb2a384`. Core, the binary's tests and docs; no app, no kit.
- The day's lock and `projects.lock` are one module, `crates/jellymoon-core/src/lock.rs`. A caller that waits removes
  only a lock it finds left behind (a plain file last written over 30 seconds from now), behind the system's lock
  on `take-over.lock` beside it, after a second look made there. Nothing at the name, a look that failed and a time
  that cannot be read are waited for. A holder past 30 seconds leaves the name alone when it lets go.
- F53's gate, notes 1 to 5: the `runs` folder a toss makes is the person's alone; `toss.md` says what a refused
  let-go can leave; tests for a copy git cannot answer for, for which refusal a wish let go gets from `run`, and for
  a let-go that waits for the day past the lock's own 3 seconds.
- The reader refuses logs that are a file, or a folder that cannot be listed, where it printed an empty day.

**The evidence.** Red first on a Mac, three runs of the old lock with a test-only pause between a look and what is
done about it: 271, 265 and 252 faults in 1200 takes of a fresh lock, and 20 faults in 24 take-overs each run. With
no pause, no fault in 3600 takes: the window is there and small. After the fix, 0 in three runs of the same tests.
Seventeen one-rule mutations were each killed by a named test; one lived (below). `cargo fmt --all --check`,
`cargo xtask check`, clippy with warnings denied and the tests of core, the binary and xtask are green at this head.

**What's next.** Windows: nothing here was run there. `wishes_made_at_once_are_all_kept_each_under_its_own_id` in
`tests/f37.rs` is the test that lost a wish in CI and is the one to watch.

**Surprising.**
- The hypothesis held: the removal after a failed look is the fault. On a Mac the look fails only when the lock
  is gone; on Windows it is taken to fail also while a removal is pending, which would be why CI saw it and local
  rounds did not. That part is read, not run.
- Two callers that both found one old lock could each remove it, the second removing the first's new lock. The
  doc had this as "not seen and not ruled out"; slowed, it shows 20 times in 24.
- A lock dated ahead of now was taken over at once. It is now left behind only when over 30 seconds ahead.
- `jellymoon run` on a wish let go with its tossed run says the wish has had its run, not that it was let go:
  that check comes first. The gate's note read the other way; the test pins what the binary says and `run.md` says so.
- One mutation lived: opening `take-over.lock` where a folder has its name is refused twice over, by the look
  that wants a plain file and by the open itself, so removing either one alone changes nothing a test can see.
- Not promised, and written down in `delivery.md`: a change that takes over 30 seconds, and a disk whose clock is
  over 30 seconds off.

**Deleted.** The lock's code in `tap.rs` (moved and rewritten) and the doc's sentences that a lock dated ahead, or
of unreadable age, is taken over. The binary's budget went from 24847 to 24931, all tests; core's from 14632 to 14891, of which 163 are the lock's tests.
