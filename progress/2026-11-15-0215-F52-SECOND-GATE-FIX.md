# F52: what its second gate found

On branch `f52-answer`, the third commit, rebased on main after the F55 follow-up (#174). The account is
`docs/development/wish.md` ("What is kept"), `run.md` ("An answer") and ADR 0018, "Found while building F52".

## What changed

- **The door, again** (`still.rs`, `mended`). A stretch of words with line breaks and no other space was read
  joined whole or not at all. A word that touched a split key id across one more break took the word's edge
  the id is found by, so `the id is`, a break, half an id, a break and its other half was kept in two pieces
  and given to the harness. A stretch is now also read with every two to eight lines in a row joined and the
  lines around them apart (`JOINED`). At most 36 cleanings of a stretch, whatever its lines: a text of 800
  two-letter lines is made in well under a second in a test's build.
- **Only marks** is now words with no letter and no digit beside the marks, so a key typed in quotes or before
  a full stop is refused as only marks.
- **A run's end reads its record before it lets go of its lock** (`run.rs`, `let_go`), not after.
- **run.md said a refused answer writes only `runs/keep.lock`.** It also makes the run's own `run.lock` where
  an older build had removed it. The sentence says so, and `f52/refused.rs` holds it to that one empty file.

## What the next session should know: open rows

1. A key split over more than eight lines is found only when no word touches it across a further break.
2. A private key's first line split inside one of its words is not found; its body is replaced only where
   its lines are a credential's shape themselves.
3. `run/never.rs` `mark` clears a wish's answer and no test fails without it (the gate's mutation 11).
4. That a toss and an answer never meet is proved from the answer's side only (`asks::for_toss`).
5. The rows of `2026-11-15-0030-F52-GATE-FIX.md`, all still open, the real harness's measurement first.
