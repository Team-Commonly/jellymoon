# F43: a tap that stops a run, and the eight findings of F42's review

- **Done.** A tap on the character of a made wish whose run is going shows one choice, "Stop it"; its tap calls
  core's `stop_run` off the window's thread and the page is drawn again with what core said
  (`app/src/stopping.rs`, `app/tests/f43.rs`, 3 tests). The eight findings of F42's review are held by
  `app/tests/f42/notes.rs`, one test in `f42/run.rs` and three kit tests. Docs: `docs/development/app.md`,
  "A wish in the window" and "A tap that stops a run".
- **The findings, and where each is held.**
  1. A project switched off has no chip from the first scene on, and no wish is made for it (`Today::offered`).
  2. The app's `--skip` names reach `run_apart`, so the run starts nothing in a folder they name.
  3. A place past the six chips is no project: the app cuts what it offers to what the kit draws.
  4. Enter calls core off the window's thread (`OnShow::wishing`); taking the answer opens no file.
  5. Words core refuses come back into the entry, unless something was typed since (`put_back_entry`).
  6. A wish's note names its words, outlives a reading that ends, and a late asking takes no newer note away.
  7. The kit keeps the chosen option by its name when the options change (`EntryState::keep_option`).
  8. Only a name core takes for `--project` is offered (`jellymoon_core::wishable`).
- **Core changed by more than one name.** `wishable(home, skip, projects)` and `going(home, day)` are new and
  public (`wish.rs`); `reader::switched_off` and `session::skipped` are `pub(crate)` so `wishable` asks the
  reader's own match. The other way was to copy the match into the app, which would be a rule out of core.
- **Decided: `layout` prints every choice.** `layout` has no tap, so it prints the "Stop it" choice of every
  made wish whose run is going, and the window shows one only after a tap. If the choice should be on the page
  with no tap, `Today::with_stops` is the one call to move into `draw`.
- **Decided: a stop is two scenes.** The choice gives way at once to "That run is being stopped." and core is
  asked off the thread, since `stop_run` waits up to 65 seconds. A run that ended meanwhile writes nothing and
  says so; a refusal is core's line.
- **Three assertions of F42's tests changed**, because a wish's note now names its words (finding 6): the
  body is compared after the name in `f42.rs` and `f42/run.rs`. `entry(None)` became `today(&world).entry(None)`
  (the function moved onto `Today`), and the wait on the run's lock became the helper `ended()`.
- **The pictures.** `examples/snapshot.rs` draws the entry, and with words the caret and the chosen chip. The
  four pictures made (empty and typed, both themes, 1440x900) show nothing overlapping or cut.
- **Not tested: what needs a person at a window.** Typing and the caret, an input method and where its
  candidate window stands, Tab, Shift-Tab and a click on a chip, Escape and a click elsewhere, `t` while
  typing, a wish drawn as soon as core answers and its star about a second after the run ends, the no-run note
  staying, typed words outliving a look's redraw, refused words coming back in the field, closing the window
  while a run is going, and the tap on a working wish's character and on "Stop it". Windows and Linux are
  CI's: the run tests were run on the Mac only.
- **Open.** A folder switched off by a name above it still has a chip (only names are on a page); its run
  starts nothing and the note says why. A wish for a project past the sixth is made with `jellymoon wish`.
- **Suggested skills.** `make-pr-easy-to-review`; this PR waits for `GATE: PASS` (a tap in a window ends an
  agent, and Enter starts one).
