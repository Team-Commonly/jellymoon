# F55: a hearing, at `jellymoon hear`

**What changed.** On branch `f55-hear`, one commit on `596007c`. Core's `hear.rs` and `hear/` turn key, ear and tick
events into what the jellyfish shows, and make a heard wish a wish after 4000 ms; `jellymoon hear` is that as a command,
`hear --settings` reads `voice.toml`, and `tests/f30.rs` holds F30's three steps. The account is
`docs/development/hear.md`; what was decided is ADR 0019, "Found while building F55". F30 stays `passes: false`.

**The evidence.** `cargo test -p jellymoon --test f55`, `--test f30`, core's `tests/hear.rs`, and the smoke chain's
`hear` step. The fixtures `hear-events.jsonl`, `hear-lines.jsonl`, `hear-runs.jsonl` and `voice.json` are the contract.

**What's next.** F56 draws a `Jelly` (seven states; `left` of 4000 is the ring; `made`, `going`, `question`) and needs
nothing else of core. F58 holds one `Hearing`: `begin(HearOptions)`, `event(HearEvent)` or `line(bytes)`, each
giving the `Jelly` when it changed; it sends a tick often enough to draw the ring, sets `HearOptions.apart` to the installed command, reads
`voice(home)` for the key, and keeps the hearing off the window's thread, since making a wish with a run waits.

**Surprising.** Final words carry no hold's number, so a late final can be taken for the next hold: F60 should number
holds. A hearing writes nothing until a wish is made, so a cut-off run reads `working` until the page is looked at.
