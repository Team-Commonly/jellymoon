# Open a wish

## Built
- **The legend says a jelly opens.** On a page with a wish, the legend row ends in one line with no mark: "tap a
  jelly to open its wish". A page with no wish (read or not) has none. The kit gains `Mark::Plain`, a legend line
  with no mark and no room kept for one (3 lines).
- **A read wish's note is true.** The old title was core's (`stands`: "This wish is being worked on." and its
  siblings); core is unchanged and now speaks for made wishes only. A wish of the reading has the app's words
  (`scene::read_said`): "This looks unfinished." / "This looks done." / "This looks like it needs you." / "This
  looks like you let it go.", then the quoted words, "You typed this in Claude Code, in <project>. Jellymoon did
  not run it." (no clause with no project), and, only when the entry offers the project a chip, "To go on with it
  here, type what you want next. It will start in <project>." A made wish's note is unchanged.
- **The tap chooses the chip.** `Showing::chip_of` gives a read wish's project; `window::poked` chooses that chip
  after the view is done with the tap (`Page::choose_option`, what `choose_waiting` uses). Typed words stay,
  nothing starts, the hold after a choice is untouched. A made wish chooses nothing. The walk's `poke` does the same.
- `standing` lost its `made` flag: it is only for made wishes now.
