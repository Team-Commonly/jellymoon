# The findings of the reviews of PR 156 and PR 157, as the coordinator ruled them

Branch `kit-follow-up`, written on `main` at `389aea7`. One commit, not pushed. The kit and the app only: nothing
in `crates/jellymoon-core`, `crates/jellymoon` or `crates/xtask`.

## From the review of PR 156 (the art removal)

- **The path check is gone** (`crates/sticker-kit/src/theme/load.rs`). `no_pictures` takes `art` out of the file
  and says the one warning; it looks at no name. `ThemeError::PathEscapes` and its line are removed. `art` in any
  shape (a table, a string, an array, a nested table, an array of tables, a number) and naming anything (`..`, a
  root, a Windows path, a script, nothing) loads the same theme with the one warning, the same on every system.
- **F32's step 4 is replaced by F49.** F32's row in `feature_list.json` is unchanged and still passes on its own
  test, whose leaving-path case now asserts that the file loads. F49 is appended: "A theme file's art is never
  read, whatever it holds, and the file loads", proved by `cargo test -p jellymoon-app --test f49`. No theme file
  is refused for what its `art` names.
- ADR 0016 has a dated correction under the sentence that said a leaving name refuses the theme. The glossary's
  **pack** says a theme file names no art.
- The four tests that ask `cx.debug_bounds` are marked `#[cfg(debug_assertions)]` (`tests/draw.rs`, three;
  `tests/entry_draw.rs`, one). No release build was made: it is reasoned from the attribute.

## From the review of PR 157 (the entry's floors)

- **A theme file never draws a pair of the entry under its floor for a key it did not set.** That is the sentence
  now, in the README, `own.rs` and `tests/floors.rs`. The earlier note's "never worse than before" was false:
  `accent_dark = "#808080"` alone moves the ring from 5.35 to 3.88, over its floor and worse.
- **A see-through `ink` or `soft` is replaced and said.** `Color::contrast` already laid a text on its ground, so
  the measure was right; what was missing is the replacement. When no field reads under the `ink` (no chip under
  the `soft`), the text itself is settled (`Side::text`): the same colour with nothing seen through where that
  reads, else black or white, with one warning for the key the file set. `ink = "#2B235020"` now loads with
  `palette.ink: #2B235020 on palette.entry #FFFFFF is under 4.5 to 1; #2B2350 is used`.
  The replaced `ink` or `soft` is the whole page's, not only the entry's: the palette has one of each.
  A see-through text that reads is kept, and a ground still gives way first where one can.
- README sentences: a file that sets only a pale `ink` or `soft` gets a different field and chips with nothing
  said; the unlit field's edge is the file's own `bubble_ring`, unchecked; one Backspace or Delete removes a whole
  cluster.
- `app/src/lib.rs`: `one_line` cuts at 300 characters (`LONGEST_LINE`), between two characters a reader sees,
  with an ellipsis. `file_part` is `one_line` and then only what follows the last `/`, `\` or `:`; the picture
  example names its picture with it.
- Tests for the four mutations that had survived: a field and a chip over a see-through paper on a dark desk
  (`tests/floors.rs`), and jamo extended-B, U+0900 and U+05C4, U+05C5, U+05C7 (`tests/entry.rs`, the backspace
  test).

## Checks

text in `tests/floors.rs`, and the cut in the app's `f32` and its unit test. Not red first, since they hold what
was already so: the named pipe in `f49`, the cluster test and the see-through paper's two cases; mutations stand
in for their red.

the example. It was run by hand with a name holding ESC, U+202E and `/../../up`, and wrote `pic-up.png` in the
folder with no escape printed.

Pictures looked at, made headless under `target/`: a theme file with `ink = "#2B235020"` and
`soft = "#5E587830"`. The typed words and both chips' names read.
