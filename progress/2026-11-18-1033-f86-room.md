# F86 follow-up: the change is on the page at 1440x900

## Why
At the default size the told note showed the did and then "+4 more": the changed lines, the only part checked
against the files, were never on the page. A typical real result is one did of two lines, one change with a
line a side, and sometimes a decided.

## Room (units, the f86 edits day at 1440x900, three notes, two placed)
- Needed for rows: did 62 + change + the count line 26. The change as two labelled rows took about 144; as one
  compact block (means 1) 82. So 170.
- Room for rows: 89 at the first step, 121 at the gap-32 step (means 2, the plan already takes it when it
  shows more of the result). Means 1 and 2 left it 49 short.
- Means 3 is not in the kit: a note's title and a choice's title share one size.
- Means 4 (approved by the coordinator): with a told note among the notes, the count of the notes left out is
  one plain line in the same words, 12 units under the last note, not a sheet with a gap. It took 101 (48 gap
  and a 53 sheet; 85 at the gap-32 step) and now takes 29. Rows get 177 of the 170 needed.

## Built
- Kit: `ToldRow.marks`, the note's own small words beside the quote of their place (`row_sub`); a marked row
  sets its quotes close (padding 3, gap 2) after the widest word. `with_last` is gone: a change is one row.
- Kit: `told::count`, the count of the notes left out: the sheet as before, or the plain line (a `more` piece)
  while a told note is among the notes. `notes.rs` calls it where it made the sheet.
- App: a change is one row, `Changed in <name>`, its before lines then its after lines, the first of each
  marked "before" and "after". A made file is `New file <name>` as before.
- Fixture `shown-told-typical.json` (did, one change, decided, nothing dropped) for the picture example.

## Red first
The typical-result test at 1440x900 failed (did only, then "+2 more") before the block and the line. The kit's
count test was red before its four recorded sheets were filled in. Those were recorded from the no-told path,
which is the code it was; the rest of the kit's suite, unchanged, also holds it.

## Asserted
- 1440x900, typical result: the did and both sides of the change are read in order, above the work's note,
  which keeps its three choices.
- 960x640: the test day's did now fits there too (asserted at both sizes); a did of 231 characters draws no row,
  and the work's note is whole.
- The pictures: `snapshot --work waiting.json --told shown-told-typical.json` and `--told shown-told.json`.
