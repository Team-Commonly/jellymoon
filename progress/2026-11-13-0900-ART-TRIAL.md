# A picture is tried before it is trusted

One PR, branch `art-trial`, after the review of #147 (GATE FAIL: three blocking, seven not). It is gated before it
merges. Finding 8 of that review (the smoke chain's programs) is not in it: it follows on its own branch,
`smoke-paths`.

## The principle

Four reviews found a picture the written rules took and the renderer choked on, and each was closed with one more
rule. The rules are now the first filter and not the proof. The first time a theme's picture is seen, the caller's
own program is started again as a child that reads it, holds it to the rules and draws it, under a limit of time.
The process that owns the window reads and draws only what such a child answered for. The decision is
`docs/adr/0016-a-picture-is-tried-before-it-is-trusted.md`.

## What was built

- `theme/trial.rs`, the parent: `Trial { program, arguments, kept, limit }`, the child started with an empty
  environment and the picture on its input, one line read back, the child killed and waited for at the limit,
  answers kept by content.
- `theme/stand.rs`, the child: `stand_trial(selects)`, called first in `main`. Rules, then
  `gpui::SvgRenderer::render_single_frame`, then `accepted <numbers> <paint> <pixels drawn>` or `refused: <why>`.
- `Theme::try_art(&trial) -> Tried { accepted, refused }`. `Accepted` has private fields and is made nowhere else;
  `Page::set_art`, `Page::with_art`, `Sprites::reset` and `headless::snapshot_with` take only that.
- The app: `main` stands as the trial first; `layout` tries the art and says each refusal on stderr; the window
  tries it on the background executor and sets it when done (`window.rs`, `arts`).
- The rules: `stroke-width` and the `viewBox` held as written numbers, no percent in a placed length, at most 40
  attributes an element counted on the text, an arc counted in a run only when it goes nowhere.

## Measured

One Mac, ten cores, a load of 10 to 18 from other sessions. Child's start included.

| | debug | release |
|---|---|---|
| Heaviest theme known inside the rules, five trials (a review's 64 round strokes of 12 dots) | 6.5 s | 0.55 s |
| The heaviest single trial of it | about 1.4 s | about 0.11 s |
| The kit's own five heaviest themes, five trials each | 0.95 to 3.1 s | 64 to 121 ms |
| A program's first start after a build | 2.4 to 4.8 s | 0.44 s, once over 1 s |

`TRIAL_LIMIT` is 20 s in a debug build and 2 s in a release. One second was tried in a release and refused an
honest picture on a helper that had just been built, so the kit's tests start their helper once before a trial
counts its time, and the limit is 2 s.

Each picture the reviews found, given to a child that draws and holds it to no rule, limit 1.5 s: the wide stroke
and the percent of a huge `viewBox` ended at the limit (1.51 to 1.83 s seen) in both builds; 60000 closes ended the
child on its stack in 10 to 57 ms; the ragged fills ended at the limit in a debug build and were drawn in time in a
release. With the rules on each is refused by its rule in 8 to 350 ms.

## Mutations

35 tried, one at a time, each put back. As the tree stands 33 are caught and 2 survive. Two more survived a first
run and are caught now: the child not drawing, and the child taking a picture over 64 KiB.

- Caught, the trial: a child over the limit not killed (the test hangs, and was stopped by hand); a kept answer
  for another picture or another key taken; a trial that ran over kept; a child's code not looked at; the picture
  read in this process when no child starts; the child given the environment; an answer with more after it; a
  limit forty times what is asked; nothing kept; the kept answer never read; the child holding nothing to the
  rules; the child saying a pixel and drawing none; an answer that drew nothing taken; the child taking a picture
  over 64 KiB.
- Caught, the rules: `stroke-width` not placed; the `viewBox` not held; a percent taken, on the root at any value,
  or on any element's size; attributes not counted; every arc counted; an arc to where it starts counted; no arc
  counted; the idle line at 1e-9; and #147's survivors T20 (`x1 y1`), T22 (`cy`), T29 (a curve's first control
  point) and C3 (the height's least side).
- Caught, the app: never standing as the trial; keeping no answer; `layout` not saying a refusal.
- **Survived.** T30, reading on past an unreadable segment: the tokenizer jumps to the end at its first error
  (`svgtypes` `path.rs:222`), so the two are one program. The child reading all it is given, not 64 KiB and a
  byte: nothing here feeds a child without end. After "the child does not draw" survived, the answer was made to
  carry the pixels drawn.

## Not closed

- Memory on macOS and Windows: the limit of time is the only bound, about a gigabyte for the worst picture known.
- The Linux memory limit (`setrlimit` through `rustix`) was not compiled here: no Linux target on this Mac. The
  Windows lint ran on the kit and the app without their window half; GPUI's build script does not cross-build.
- A machine so busy an honest picture runs over: refused for that run, not kept, tried at the next start.
- Handles another library left inheritable are inherited; closing them takes `unsafe`.
- The file name of a kept answer is 64 bits of `DefaultHasher`; the whole picture is compared, so a clash costs a
  trial and no more.
- #147's survivors C9, C15, C16, C18, C19, C20 and C25 are in the pricing and the reach. No test was written for
  them: they are what the next change removes.
