# F37: a typed wish is kept on the day's page in the person's exact words

- **The command.** `jellymoon wish --say <words> --project <name> [--date <day>]` keeps a made wish in
  `pages/<day>.wishes.json` as `m1` to `m8`, working, and prints `{wish, run: null, why}`. Nothing is started.
  The rules are core's `wish.rs`; `docs/development/wish.md` says all of it.
- **Reused, not copied.** The day's lock is the tap's, now one function (`tap::locked`) for both; the door
  (`plain`) and the write (`put`) are the tap's too. The words go through the ruling's own cleaning.
- **The limits, each a one-line refusal that writes nothing.** Empty once cleaned; over 200 characters (never cut);
  a ninth wish in a day; a project that is not one plain folder name; made wishes that cannot be used (never
  written over); a day locked for 3 seconds.
- **On the page.** Core's `with_made` puts them after the reading's: `jellymoon page`, and the app on every scene
  of the day. The note "wished here today" (the kit's new `Note::Whole`) draws each one's words whole or counts it.
- **For F39.** A made wish cannot be taken back. A project name of 40 letters and digits with no break reads as a
  credential and is refused. `Wished.run` is a string id, still always null. Not proved: Windows modes; sixteen wishes in the kit.
