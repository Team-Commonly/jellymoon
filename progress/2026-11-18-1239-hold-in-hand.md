# Hold in hand: work waits three looks after the page left the person's hand

## What was built
- `Turns::asks` (`app/src/showing.rs`): a look made while a note is in the person's hand (`working::in_hand`)
  sets the hold to `HOLD` (3), every turn kept as before. So work comes no sooner than the fourth look after the
  page left the hand by any path: a tap, or a look that took a stop's choice away when the run ended by itself, or
  a clash's choice that a reading or a ruling made elsewhere took away. `acted()` stays for the acts that leave
  the page out of hand. A page that stays in hand showed no work before either; a tap on a character still opens
  work at once (`poked` does not read the hold).
- `docs/development/app.md`: the rule as its own bullet ("Not right after the page left the hand"); the
  "Never over a note in the person's hand", "Not right after the person chose" and "What stays" bullets wrapped
  at 120 columns.
- `progress/2026-11-18-1206-work-shows-hold.md`: its budget line read 14385 to 14497; on the landed chain it went
  14472 to 14584.

  HOLD` after `typing` on a note with a field, after `answered(None)`, and after a clash's `chose`. Green from the
