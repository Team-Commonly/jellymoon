# Typed ahead (2026-11-24 18:08 UTC)

Branch `typed-ahead`, on `1baa2ec`. No row of `feature_list.json` changes.

## Why
On the released build a person typed a wish, pressed Enter and typed the next wish at once. The entry took no key
until core had answered, so the first characters of the second wish were dropped with no sign, and that wish was
made with what was left. Taking no key between Enter and the answer was a decision (the kit's `README.md`,
`docs/development/app.md`). It is changed here, on purpose.

## Built
- **`EntryState`** (`crates/sticker-kit/src/entry.rs`). Enter gives what it gave (`Made`), keeps the words aside
  (`owed`) and leaves the typed text empty. Every key is taken while the answer is owed: text, a paste, a
  composition, the editing keys, the caret, another option. Only Enter gives nothing then, and what was typed
  since stays. `waiting()` is still the question "is an answer owed"; `owed()` is the words it is owed for.
- **The answer.** Kept: the words kept aside are forgotten, and what was typed since stays with its caret. Not
  kept, nothing typed since: the words are back as typed, the caret at their end. Not kept, something typed
  since: the words come back in front of it, one space between, as much of them as the cap leaves room for
  beside what was typed since, which is never cut; the caret is at the end, and a composition keeps its own
  caret and its marked characters. An option chosen since stays.
- **Drawing** (`draw/entry.rs`). While an answer is owed and nothing was typed since, the field paints the words
  kept aside in the hint's grey, the caret where an entry that shows its hint has it. With the first key the
  typed text is painted in ink. The line kept for a click and for the input method is of the typed text only.
  `go` is not lit while an answer is owed.
- **The platform's text input** (`draw/input.rs`) is accepted while an answer is owed.
- **A picture of it**: `Typing::since` (`draw/headless.rs`) and `--since <more words>` of the app's snapshot
  example.

## Every reader of `waiting()`
| Where | What it does now |
|---|---|
| `EntryState::enter` | gives nothing while an answer is owed, and changes nothing |
| `EntryState::holds` | reads `owed` itself: an entry that is owed an answer holds words, so it is never taken off its page |
| `EntryView::asked` (`draw/entry.rs`) | unchanged: an answer finds its field by the note's id Enter was pressed under |
| `EntryView::shape`, `paint` (`draw/entry.rs`) | no longer read it: pale only for the words kept aside with nothing typed since; the caret is always painted for the field with the keys |
| `Page::entry_key` (`draw/entry.rs`) | the early return is gone: every key is taken, Escape too |
| `Page::entry_go` (`draw/entry.rs`) | reads `holds()` alone: a tap on `go` while an answer is owed is Enter, which gives nothing, and is no `Tap::Go` |
| `Page::layers` (`draw/mod.rs`) | unchanged: `go` is lit only with words and no answer owed; the hint is hidden while words are held or owed |
| `fit` (`draw/field.rs`) | unchanged: a field that is owed its answer is kept apart, and first |
| `accepts_text_input` (`draw/input.rs`) | no longer reads it: true whenever the line with the keys is there |
| the app | has no reader outside its tests: `Showing::waiting` is another thing (the project a first page waits for) |
