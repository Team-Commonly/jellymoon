# F56: the jellyfish is drawn from core's state with the kit's edge scene

On branch `f56-jelly`, one commit on top of `2c29042`. The account is `docs/development/app.md` ("The jellyfish"),
`hear.md` ("What is drawn") and ADR 0019, "Found while building F56".

## What changed

- **Core** (`hear/drawn.rs`): `JellyState::ORDER`; `jellyfish(line, home, day)`, the one line drawn of a hearing's
  line and the day's made wishes; `Jelly::read`, the door a line passes; `Jelly::drawn()`, a `JellyDrawn` with the
  bubble's texts, whose words each is, a count and the ring in thousandths. Example: `jelly-drawn.jsonl`.
- **The kit** (`edge.rs`, `layout/edge.rs`): `Edge`, with a mood, a count, a bubble of texts with a tone and a
  ring; `layout_edge` in two windows of fixed sizes; `place_corner` and `place_beside`, given the screen and the
  room as numbers; a `quoted` role for someone else's words.
- **The app** (`jelly.rs`): `jellymoon-app layout --jelly <file, or -> --screen <W>x<H>`, with `--theme` and
  `--date`. It prints windows and pieces and opens nothing. The smoke chain has a `jelly` step.

## What the next session should know: open rows

1. Nothing is painted: the kit's `draw` knows no edge piece, and no theme has an ink for `quoted`. F57.
2. `layout --jelly` gives the whole screen as the room. F57 asks the system for the room and gives it to the same
   two functions.
3. A line in `working`, `asks` or `waits` is drawn only when the day says so now; without `--date` it is drawn as
   given, since nothing but the input is read.
4. The kit's tests for the placing and the cut were written after the kit's code, and held by mutations only.
5. One build of this unit ran with the default cargo home, not the pinned one, because the two exports stood after
   an `&&` chain whose first step failed. It fetched the crates over the network into a new folder in the home
   directory; the run was stopped and the folder removed. Put the exports first and alone, never after `&&`.
