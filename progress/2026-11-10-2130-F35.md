# F35: a tap on a clash settles it, and the ruling reaches the person's running sessions

- **What a tap does** is `app/src/taps.rs`: `Today::tapped`, called by the window's handler and by the new
  `jellymoon-app tap`. A clash's option calls core's `rule` with that clash's two statements; "Turn on delivery"
  calls core's `install_hook`. Core gained `hook_installed` (`jellymoon hook status`); nothing else in core changed.
- **Settled.** Which side stands is kept in `pages/<day>.ruled.json`, by the clash's two statements. The kit strikes
  only a choice's first option, so the replaced statement is drawn first and the one that stands second.
- **Trust.** The words of a ruling are the page data's, kept page included; `delivery.md`, "A tap in the app".
- **Delivery note** sits before the clashes: a full page hides a clash behind "+1 more", never the note.
- **Proof.** `app/tests/f35.rs` (5 cases); smoke `tap`, `tap-told`, `today-settled`; `hook status` in `f29.rs`.
- **Not proved.** The window's own path from a click to `after_tap` (the kit's click test stops at its callback;
  the glue in `window.rs` was not run with a click). A model that hears two opposite rulings in one step.
- **Next.** A page that says whether a session heard a ruling; a ruling only for the clash's project.
