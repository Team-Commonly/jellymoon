# F28 · the first review's findings: a door for the app and the kit (2026-11-10)

**What changed.** Page data and theme files are bounded where they come in. The app refuses a page file over 1 MiB
and draws the first 24 wishes, 8 clashes, 12 stars a wish and 8 unknown items, with a first note that counts the rest.
The kit refuses a theme whose numbers are not finite or out of range, lays out 12 notes and counts the others on a
last sheet (`+N more of M notes`), and returns at most 5000 pieces. `[forbidden]` takes `gpui*`.

**Evidence.** `make preflight` green. F28 at the binary: 13 tests before, 17 after. The reviewer's shapes on the
debug binary: 40k unknown items 0.05 s (was 3.0), 10k clashes 0.05 s, 8k paused wishes 0.05 s; 20k clashes and 40k
paused wishes are over 1 MiB and refused in 0.01 s. A theme with `height = inf` or `3e7` is refused by key.

**What's next.** A second fresh-context review. A design question for F29 or after: two clashes with the longest
quotes do not both fit beside the list, so the second is counted, not drawn (`docs/development/app.md`).
