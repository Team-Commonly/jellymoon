# The eight findings of #139's after-merge review

One ungated PR, branch `art-size`. Nothing here changes what leaves the machine or what an agent may do on it.

## The principle

The art door accepts only what it reads the way the renderer does, and refuses the rest. Its counts bound what a
file costs only while the renderer draws the picture the door counted. #139 closed one place where the two read
a file differently (a side left out) and left the others open. `crates/sticker-kit/README.md` states the rule;
`tests/alike.rs` holds each case, and `tests/draw.rs` rasterises the roots.

## What changed

- **1, the blocking one.** The door read names in any case; the renderer reads `viewBox`, `width` and `height` in
  that case only. `viewbox="0 0 8 8"`, `WIDTH="8" HEIGHT="8"`, `viewBox="- - 8 8"` and `viewBox="0.0.0.0 0 8 8"` were
  counted as 8 by 8 (paint 64) and drawn 8192 by 8192 in 4.3 to 5.3 s. Now every name is matched as SVG writes
  it, a `viewBox` is four plain numbers (a `-` before the first two only), and a `<svg` that states a size twice
  is refused.
- **2 and 3.** What made a curve slow was its reach, not its kind (the times are in the README). A path stays
  within 512 of the picture's corner (`MAX_ART_REACH`), with the pen followed through relative commands and the
  handle an `s` or `t` mirrors held too. Each kind of command has its own count in `theme/path_cost.rs`: an arc's
  numbers and a `t`'s count two, the rest one. `ARC_NUMBERS` is gone. An arc's flags run into a number (`11 2`)
  are refused: the renderer reads two flags and a 2 there, a reader of numbers an 11 and a 2.
- **4.** `Theme::art_svgs` gives every piece from one pass, each file read once, one sum. `Theme::art_svg`, which
  `Sprites::reset` called five times, is gone.
- **5.** `cargo xtask smoke` also refuses `--config`, `--manifest-path`, `-r` inside a cluster (`-vr`), and what
  builds only some targets (`--bin`, `--lib`, `--example`, `--test`, `--bench`, their plurals, `--exclude`): the
  chain runs `jellymoon`, `jellymoon-app` and `fake-claude`, and one would be left from an earlier build. `-p`,
  `--package` and `--workspace` only add, and are taken. The build starts without `CARGO_BUILD_TARGET` and
  `CARGO_BUILD_TARGET_DIR`.
- **6.** A home is `smoke-home-<pid>-<nanoseconds>`, so no later run is given the name of a home an earlier one
  left, and unlinking a lock file after removing its home takes nothing from a living run. The homes are removed
  before the build, so a failed build has still removed what a stopped `--real` run left.
- **7.** The kept page's test stamps its lines at noon of the day in the system's zone (with `jiff`, already the
  app's) and runs the program that keeps the first page in that zone. Passed under `TZ=Pacific/Kiritimati`,
  `Pacific/Auckland`, `Etc/GMT+12` and unset.
- **8.** `bundle.sh` refuses a `MIN_MACOS` of `0` or with a leading zero.

## Measured (a debug build, one busy Mac: the spread is the machine's)

One picture, 8 wide, drawn 256, a thin stroke, all 1024 counted numbers on one kind at a reach of 512: lines
0.10 s, arcs 0.12 s, `C` 0.11 to 0.17 s, `S` 0.12 to 0.27 s, `Q` 0.07 to 0.15 s, `T` 0.15 to 0.18 s. Within about
twice the lines, not equal. The whole page with five such files: 8.3 s curved, 8.4 s with lines.

## Not done, and why

- A thin-stroked `circle` or `ellipse` of radius 4096 costs about 13 ms and holds no path numbers. Only the 100
  elements bound it (about 1.3 s a file). It was so before #139, and a fix is a rule on shapes, not on paths.
- A `build.target` in a `.cargo/config.toml` above the repo still moves the build. `smoke.md` says so.
- No test forces the race of finding 4 or of finding 6: each is held by construction (one pass; a name used once)
  and by a test of the construction.
- The red for findings 1 and 7 was seen in the review's own run, not run again before the fix.
- `CLAUDE.md` is not touched. Its "no `sh` in tests" rule has one exception in the repo, `app/tests/bundle.rs`,
  and should say so. Proposed: "no `sh` in tests, but for the test of a shell script that is itself for one
  system: it is `cfg`'d to that system and says why."
- Fixtures changed because the door is narrower: `tests/limits.rs` and `tests/cost.rs` step by 0 where they
  stepped 1000 times by 1.
